ESC setup

In the ESC’s own app, box or card — the flight controller cannot change these.

⚠️ Blades off / motor unplugged while programming.

1Governor: let Rotorflight do it

Set the ESC’s governor to OFF / external governor. Rotorflight’s governor holds the head speed.

Two governors fighting = surging head speed. One governor only. (Prefer the ESC’s? Then turn Rotorflight’s off in the Governor step.)

2Throttle range

Rotorflight drives the ESC with a 1000–2000 µs throttle. Teach the ESC that range with the calibration below — from this phone, no computer.

Wrong range = motor never starts, or never reaches full head speed. (Scorpion shows the learned values read-only in Sproto: about 1.00 / 1.00 / 2.00 ms when done.)

⚡Throttle calibration — from here

⚠️ Blades OFF or pinion out. Flight pack UNPLUGGED. Flight controller on USB power. Transmitter OFF.

The phone holds the motor channel at full throttle; you plug the battery; the ESC learns the top; then the phone drops to minimum and it learns the bottom. The hold renews every half second and lapses by itself a second after the phone stops — it cannot get stuck on.

3Telemetry ON

Enable the ESC’s telemetry output and pick the protocol that matches the brand you choose in First-time basics (Scorpion, Hobbywing, YGE…).

Voltage, current, temperature and head speed all ride this one wire. Head speed is what the governor governs.

4Spool-up

Set the ESC’s soft start short (or off). Rotorflight’s governor manages the gentle spool-up.

A long ESC soft start on top of the governor’s ramp makes spool-up sluggish and confuses the head-speed loop.

5BEC voltage

Set the BEC to what your servos are rated for (6.0 V, 7.4 V or 8.4 V).

Too high cooks servos; too low makes them slow. The flight controller runs happily on any of these.

6Motor timing & brake

Timing per your motor maker’s advice (usually auto or low). Brake off.

Then note the motor’s pole count — First-time basics needs it for the head-speed maths.

Done? Back in the wizard, First-time basics picks the ESC’s brand for telemetry, and the Governor step sets the head speeds.
