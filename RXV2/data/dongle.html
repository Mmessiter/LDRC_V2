Rotorflight dongle

the phone app on a Nexus that another receiver flies

loading…
Any radio can fly the model; this board only talks to the flight controller.
Wired to a spare Rotorflight UART set to MSP, it speaks plain MSP to the flight controller and Bluetooth or WiFi to your phone. Every page works as on the receiver. No radio, no channel output, nothing to bind.

Wiring

5 Vflight controller 5 V
GNDflight controller GND
D5flight controller TX (its data comes in here)
D6flight controller RX

In Rotorflight (Ports), set that UART to MSP at the speed below. The receiver port stays with whatever receiver flies the model.
