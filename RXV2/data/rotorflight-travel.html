Swash extents (degrees)

Collective is how much pitch the blades get at full stick — the fix for “nowhere near enough collective”. The total limit caps cyclic + collective combined; the flight controller keeps it at least 6° above the collective limit, so it may adjust your number on save.

Calibration (gains)

These make the degrees MEAN degrees: the gain scales how far the swash really moves per commanded degree. If a pitch gauge reads less than the limit says (e.g. 6° real when 14° is commanded), multiply the gain by the ratio (here ×14/6). Direction is preserved — only the amount changes. Re-measure after saving; if the reading stops growing, the servo travel is the ceiling — stop and investigate before forcing it. Collective up/down balance evens up unequal positive vs negative collective (linkage geometry): if up reads less than down, step it one way, re-measure, and reverse if the gap widens. (Rotorflight calls this “geo correction” — nothing to do with GPS.)

Swash trims (degrees)

Small levelling corrections for the swashplate — use to cure a steady hover drift after mechanical setup is close.

Tail travel

Degrees of tail-blade pitch each way, and the centre offset.
