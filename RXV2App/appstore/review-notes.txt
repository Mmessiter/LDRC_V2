HOW TO REVIEW THIS APP WITHOUT THE HARDWARE

This app configures a model-helicopter receiver that I design and build myself,
so you will not have one. You do not need one.

On launch, the app scans by Bluetooth and finds nothing. Below the "Searching"
spinner are two buttons:

    "No receiver yet? Try the receiver demo"
    "Try the dongle demo"

Tap either. The whole app then runs against a simulated helicopter, with every
page live and editable. That is the intended way to review it, and it needs no
hardware, no account and no sign-in. Bluetooth may be denied and the demo still
works.

WHAT IT IS
The LDRC RX V2 is a radio-control receiver for model helicopters. The app talks
to it over Bluetooth LE using a standard GATT service — no MFi accessory, no
External Accessory framework. It sets flight-controller parameters, reads flight
logs, and can send the receiver a firmware update.

ABOUT THE WEB VIEW (guideline 4.2 and 2.5.2)
Part of the interface is drawn by a web view, and I would rather explain that up
front than have it look like a repackaged website:

  * Every page is BUNDLED INSIDE THIS BINARY (see webroot/ in the app). Nothing
    is loaded from any website. There is no server involved.
  * In this build, HTML and JavaScript are NEVER fetched from anywhere at run
    time. Only data crosses Bluetooth — JSON to and from the user's own receiver.
  * There is no self-update mechanism of any kind in the iOS app.
  * Firmware images can only be downloaded over HTTPS from messiter.com, my own
    site; the host is hard-coded and any other URL is refused. Those images go to
    the user's receiver over Bluetooth. They never execute on iOS.

Native, outside the web view: the Bluetooth scanner with live signal strength
and a too-weak-to-connect warning, per-model photographs from the camera and the
photo picker, backup files exported and imported through the share sheet and the
Files app, and offline replay of a recorded session.

SAFETY
The app writes stored settings only. It cannot arm the model, spin the motor or
move a control surface in real time, and it refuses to change anything while the
flight controller reports itself armed.

PRIVACY
No accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no data collection whatsoever. Settings
and photographs stay in the app's own storage on the phone.
