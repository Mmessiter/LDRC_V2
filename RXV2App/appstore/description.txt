RXV2 sets up a radio-control model helicopter from your phone. No laptop, no computer program, no cables to a PC — at the flying field, or on the kitchen table.

YOU NEED THE HARDWARE
This app is a companion to LockDown Radio Control hardware. It needs either an LDRC RX V2 receiver or an LDRC dongle to do anything useful.

Without one, the app opens in DEMO MODE, where every page works against a simulated helicopter. Look around the whole app before you build anything.

The hardware is not for sale. This is an open project — the circuit designs, the firmware and both phone apps are free, and you build your own. Everything you need is at messiter.com.

IT WORKS WITH THE V1 TRANSMITTER
The RX V2 receiver flies with the original LockDown Radio Control transmitter, built on a Teensy 4.1. That transmitter's source is on GitHub, free, for anyone to build:
github.com/Mmessiter/LockDownRadioControl

WHAT IT DOES
• Sets up a new helicopter step by step, from an empty flight controller to ready to fly
• Servos, swashplate, blade pitch and travel limits, with a bench hold that drives the swash to exact angles for you
• Head speed and governor, per flight bank
• Rates and PIDs, with sliders that say what they change rather than what they are called
• Rescue, switches, telemetry and in-flight adjustments
• Reads the flight controller's black box and shows you the vibrations, then offers the filter change — with limits, a read-back and an undo
• Flight traces: head speed, governor, throttle, battery and attitude over time, with a plain-English summary
• Backs up the whole setup to your phone, and puts it back
• Updates the receiver's firmware over Bluetooth

PLAIN ENGLISH
Every label says what the knob actually does, with the firmware's own term in brackets for anyone who wants it. "Roll wind-up relief (I-term relax cutoff)". A search finds any setting by either name.

SAFE ON THE BENCH
Setting up happens with the blades off or the motor unplugged. The app refuses to change anything while the model is armed, and it never spins a motor or moves a servo of its own accord.

FREE, AND FREE SOFTWARE
The app is free. No adverts, no accounts, no subscriptions, no tracking, and nothing at all collected about you — everything stays on your phone and your own receiver.
