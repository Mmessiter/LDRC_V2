Wiring

Each plug, one line. Get these right and everything after is easy.

⚠️ Blades off / motor unplugged while wiring.
⚠️ Do not power the servos yet (leave them unplugged, or the system unpowered) until the Servos step has set each one’s operating frequency and the BEC voltage matches — wrong settings can damage a servo.

S1 S2 S3Swashplate servos

S1 = the front-or-back servo (the elevator one).

Then go clockwise around the swash, seen from above: next is S2, then S3.

The mixer assumes this order — swap two plugs and the swash tilts the wrong way.

S4Tail servo

The tail (rudder) servo goes on S4.

Motor-driven tail? Leave S4 empty.

ESCThrottle lead

The ESC’s servo-style plug goes to the ESC port.

Carries the throttle signal — and on most setups the ESC’s BEC powers everything through it.

TLMESC telemetry wire

The ESC’s single data wire goes to the TLM pad.

Brings voltage, current, temperature — and head speed on digital ESCs (Hobbywing, Scorpion…). Pick the ESC’s brand later in First-time basics.

RPMRPM wire (if the ESC has one)

A separate RPM wire goes to the RPM pad.

The governor’s head-speed signal. Skip if head speed already comes on the telemetry wire.

RXThis receiver

Plug it into the default receiver port (labelled ELRS / RX) — it listens for CRSF out of the box.

Any other port? Then the configurator must set it up first (USB, once).

TX→RX, RX→TX, plus power. Done when the Rotorflight button appears on the front screen.
