Wake the ESC up

A Scorpion only listens for a programmer in its first seconds after power-up. The flight controller is now asking every 200 ms — give the ESC a fresh start:

  1. Unplug the flight battery.
  2. Wait 5 seconds.
  3. Plug it back in and leave this page open.

If the flight controller is on USB it keeps asking while the ESC boots. On battery only, it reboots too — that usually works as well, it just takes 10–15 s longer.
