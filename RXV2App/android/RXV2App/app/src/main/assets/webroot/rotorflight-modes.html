Switches and knobs

what each switch or knob of the transmitter makes the flight controller do

Rotorflight keeps these on two tabs of its configurator, Modes (the switch jobs) and Adjustments (the bank and rates selectors, and a knob that changes a setting). Many found that confusing — the bank selector is a switch job to most of us — so this page holds them all, and writes each to its own table out of sight. The transmitter’s own Switches & knobs page does the same.

Knobs and switches that change a setting

a knob or a switch changes one Rotorflight setting while you fly (Rotorflight: Adjustments)

Connect a USB cable between the flight controller’s USB socket and the receiver’s USB socket. The knobs and settings need the USB connection.

Once saved they are kept by the flight controller: they keep working with the USB unplugged.
