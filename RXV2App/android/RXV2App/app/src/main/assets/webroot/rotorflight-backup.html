Backup & restore

The whole Rotorflight setup — safe on the phone.

Backing up…
Keep the phone close to the model until the backup is complete. Don't switch the transmitter ON or the model OFF.
Restoring…
⚠️ Keep the transmitter OFF and the model ON until this finishes.

Backup lives in the app (over Bluetooth) — the phone is where the backup is kept. Open this page from the LDRC app to use it.
