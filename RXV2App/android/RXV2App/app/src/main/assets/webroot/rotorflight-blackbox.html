When to record (Blackbox mode, device, rate)

Record whenever the motor is armed, to the flight controller’s memory, 1 loop in 8. No switch needed — every flight is kept until you erase.

What to record (Blackbox fields)

Tap Recommended set, then Save. That is Rotorflight’s usual fields plus ESC and Governor, so head-speed problems show up. Compass and GPS only if fitted.
