Calibrate

teach the flight controller which way is level (IMU / accelerometer)

🎉 From here on everything is done from your phone via the receiver. The computer will not be needed again.

Already calibrated in the configurator? The numbers below should read near 0° — tick this step and move on.

Best done flat on a desk, BEFORE the board goes into the model — the helicopter may not sit level. Keep it perfectly still; takes 2 seconds. Front-or-back mounting does not affect it.
