Set credentials

Saved to NVS and used on next boot. Edit either field and tap save; the current values are pre-filled.

Tip: WiFi is steadiest with the transmitter switched off. The control radio and WiFi share the 2.4 GHz band, so a live transmitter link can make WiFi drop in and out. Use the transmitter or the phone when you can — both together works, but expect WiFi to be less consistent.

What is AP mode? AP (“access point”) means the receiver makes its own little WiFi network that your phone joins directly — no home router, no internet, just your phone talking straight to the receiver.

Tick this at the flying field. Away from home, your house WiFi is out of range, so the receiver would otherwise waste every start-up trying to find it. Ticked, it skips that and is ready straight away on its own network.

After you save, your phone won't follow automatically — you'll need to open your phone's WiFi settings and join the receiver's network (the page will tell you the name). Untick it at home to go back to your normal WiFi.

Factory defaults

Use this before passing the receiver to someone else. Wipes the saved WiFi password, model name, bind state, output protocol and any Rotorflight backups. The receiver reboots and comes up as a fresh unit at RXV2.local.
