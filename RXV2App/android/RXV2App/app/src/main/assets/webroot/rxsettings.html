Receiver battery

Measured through a divider on D9. 220 k/10 k = ratio 23.0 (to 12S); 100 k/10 k = 11.0 (to 6S).

Automatic fly mode

WiFi and Bluetooth switch off by themselves about a second after arming, on the ground. Disarming brings them back. 🔒 Always on with Rotorflight.
