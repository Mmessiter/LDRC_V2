Hello wave & flight stamping

Channels that wiggle when Bluetooth comes up. Comma list; blank = none. On a Rotorflight heli use ch 1.

Battery voltage (voltage divider)

A divider on D9. 220 k/10 k = ratio 23.0 (to 12S); 100 k/10 k = 11.0 (to 6S).

Automatic fly mode

WiFi and Bluetooth switch off by themselves 3 s after arming, on the ground. Disarming brings them back. 🔒 Always on with Rotorflight.

…

Where every channel goes if the link is lost in flight. Set the sticks and switches safe, then save. On CRSF, Rotorflight’s own failsafe overrules this.
