Realistic spool-up (helicopters)
When the motor comes back on, the head speed builds like a real governor — with a stab of tail while it accelerates. Two speeds, chosen automatically: a brief cut (bailing out of an auto mid-air) recovers quickly (¼ of the time below); after a long cut (landed, sitting in the hold) you get the full slow ground spool. Cutting the motor stays instant.
Motor / governor channel (NOT the collective stick! 0 = off) High = motor OFF (inverted channel) Ground spool-up time (seconds; brief cuts recover in ¼ of this) Tail stab while spooling (µs, minus = other way) Rudder channel Pulse mode (Malcolm’s manual PWM: blips the motor on/off with a rising on-share, so neXt’s own rotor inertia turns it into a real head-speed climb — for sims that treat the motor as a pure switch) Pulse period (ms — shorter = smoother, longer = chunkier; try 250)
Which channel is the motor? Open View channels, flip in and out of your autorotation bank, and watch which bar jumps between low and high — that’s the one. It must be the channel neXt’s motor follows, never the collective stick.
