Before you start

A computer only to flash the firmware. Everything else from the phone, over the USB cable.

✓First, get a short USB-C data cable

Connect both wires: the receiver’s lead and a short USB-C cable from the receiver to the flight controller. The lead flies the model and carries the everyday pages. The USB cable is the configurator’s own channel: every setting of any size, Ports, In-flight adjustments, the command line, a factory reset and the black box, all from the phone — everything the configurator can do, except flashing.

About 70 mm, right-angled at both ends if you can (a Chenyang CY-UC-088-0.07M), and it must carry data: many short USB-C leads only charge. Without the USB cable the computer is needed for a couple more tasks below (the port, CRSF and telemetry), and In-flight adjustments, the command line and a factory reset stay out of reach.

1Update the firmware (computer)

USB lead from the flight controller to your computer, Rotorflight Configurator, Firmware Flasher tab: flash the latest Rotorflight.

New boards do fly as delivered — but while the cable is in, start current. A warning that calibration is still needed can be ignored: the wizard’s next step does it from the phone. Flashing is the one job the phone cannot do: it needs the board in bootloader mode on a computer.

!If the update sticks or fails

Usually a bad USB lead. The board is not dead — its bootloader lives in permanent memory and cannot be erased.

1. Unplug USB and battery. 2. Hold the little BOOT button on the flight controller, plug the USB in while holding it, then release. 3. The configurator now shows DFU (top right) — go to the Firmware Flasher, tick Full chip erase, and flash again.

Use a short, known-good data cable straight into the computer (no hub) — charge-only leads are the classic trap: they power the board but carry no data.

2Tell it where the receiver is (phone)

Plug the receiver’s lead into a spare port. Connect a short USB-C data cable from the receiver’s USB socket to the flight controller’s. Power the model and open this app: the Rotorflight button appears. Open Ports, choose receiver for the socket the lead is in, and Save.

📡 Open Ports →

That one Save sets the port, CRSF, serial receiver mode and telemetry, and restarts the flight controller. The lead is then live; the cable can stay in or come out. With the dongle instead of the receiver, the Ports page is the same.

orNo USB cable? The configurator way

While still on the computer. Configuration tab: the port the receiver is on set to serial receiver. Receiver tab: Receiver Mode Serial, Provider CRSF, Telemetry ON. Then Save & Reboot.

Without telemetry the board is mute on the receiver port — nothing reaches it until the configurator switches telemetry on and restarts it.

After that restart, everything else is done from here. The computer can go away.
