Before you start

One USB session on a computer — three tasks the receiver cannot do.

Connect a USB lead from the flight controller to your computer and run the Rotorflight Configurator. The receiver need not be connected yet. Then:

1Update the firmware

Flash the latest Rotorflight (Firmware Flasher tab).

New boards do fly as delivered — but while the cable is in, start current. After flashing, the configurator may warn that calibration is still needed — ignore it (the wizard’s next step does it from the phone), or calibrate right there while still plugged in: quick and easy.

2Check the serial port

On the Configuration tab: the port where this receiver is plugged in must be the one Rotorflight expects (the serial receiver port).

The board only listens on the port named here — a mismatch means silence.

3Enable Telemetry

On the Receiver tab: turn Telemetry ON, then Save & Reboot (button, bottom right).

Without telemetry the board is mute — it cannot even be discovered.

After that reboot, the Rotorflight button appears on this phone — and everything else is done from here. The computer can go away.
