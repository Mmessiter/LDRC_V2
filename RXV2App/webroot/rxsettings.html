Head-speed gear ratio

Motor turns per rotor turn. Head-speed telemetry = motor RPM ÷ this. 1.0 = direct drive.

Battery voltage (voltage divider)

Measures the pack through a divider on D9 (GPIO 8), the spare pad on 2-radio boards. 220 kΩ over 10 kΩ = ratio 23.0, good to 12S; 100 k/10 k = 11.0, good to 6S. Add 100 nF pin→GND. Volts go to the transmitter as telemetry. With FC telemetry switched off, the receiver finds the divider by itself on D9 — the one free pad on 2-radio boards (D4 carries the status LED).

Automatic fly mode

The safety net for forgetting Fly now: 3 seconds after the model is armed — still on the ground, during spool-up — the receiver switches its WiFi and Bluetooth off by itself — nothing interferes with the radio link. Disarming brings everything back (so does switching the transmitter off after landing). With this on, the front page hides the Fly now button — not needed any more. On Rotorflight the arming channel is found automatically from the flight controller; for other setups, set it on the Output protocol page.

Failsafe positions (signal loss)

…

Used when the transmitter link is lost in flight: the receiver drives every channel to the positions you save here. Set the model where you want it on signal loss (motor off / disarmed), then tap Save. Note: on CRSF the flight-controller (e.g. Rotorflight) runs its own failsafe and overrules this — set that in its Failsafe tab. This applies on SBUS / FBUS / IBUS / PPM models. To capture positions, first open View channels and set the sticks/switches, then come here and save. (A no-transmitter power-up is always disarmed-safe regardless.)
