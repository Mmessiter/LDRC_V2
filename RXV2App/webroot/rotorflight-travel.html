Swash extents (degrees)

Pitch at full stick. The total limit caps cyclic + collective together, and the flight controller may raise it on save.

Calibration (gains)

Control sense
Moves the wrong way? Tick to reverse just that control — servos and the others stay untouched.

Scales how far the swash really moves per commanded degree. Gauge reads 6° when 14° is asked? Multiply the gain by 14/6.

Bench measure (swash calibration)

Motor off, blades clear. Drives the swash to an exact angle with the gyros bypassed. The flight controller will not arm while this is on.

Measuring cyclic gain: hold 8° of Roll, rotate the head by hand and take the peak gauge reading. Peak 5.6° → new gain = current × 8 ÷ 5.6.

Swash trims (%)

Levelling corrections, in percent of servo throw. Cures a steady hover drift.

Tail travel
