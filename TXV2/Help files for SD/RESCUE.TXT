Rescue: what Rotorflight does when you flick the rescue switch. The names, order and units are those of the Rotorflight configurator's Rescue Settings. It needs a Version 2 receiver with Bluetooth, the safety on and the model disarmed.||

The values are those of the bank shown at the top left: change bank with the bank switch, and the page reads again.||

Enable Rescue: Off or On. Tap to change. (Whether rescue climbs or holds a height is set on the Height hold page.)||

Flip to upright: Flip or No-Flip. If the model is upside down when rescue engages, Flip rolls it level-side-up first. Recommended Flip. Tap to change.||

Pull-up, Climb and Hover Collective: percent of full collective for each stage (0 to 100, halves allowed).||

Pull-up Time, Climb Time, Flip Fail Time and Exit Time: seconds, in tenths (1.5 = a second and a half).||

Leveling Gain and Flip-to-Upright Gain: how firmly it levels and how hard it rolls over (5 to 250).||

Max Levelling Rate (degrees a second, 1 to 1000) and Max Leveling Accel. (1 to 10000): how fast the model may be rolled level.||

Next > opens the second page: the rescue mode (Climb or Hold height), the hover height, its P, I and D, and the maximum collective. < Previous comes back, with your edits kept.||

Press a number to type a new one. Save sends the values to the flight controller, stores them there, and reads them back: the page then shows what the flight controller holds. OK leaves; if something was edited and not saved, it asks.||
