How Rotorflight cleans vibration out of the gyro signal, with|
the phone app's names. The same in every bank ('All banks' at|
the top left).||
It needs the safety on (model disarmed) and a Version 2|
receiver with Bluetooth. With no model, the pages show the|
model's backup on the card, if one was made: a 'Save' then|
changes the backup, and the change is offered to the model|
when it next connects.||
Tap a number to type a new one. Tap Filter type or Strength|
to step to the next choice.|
Each section has an Enable (green = on). Off hides its rows.|
'Next >' opens 'Filters: expert mode', the rarely needed extras.|
'Save' appears once something is changed. It writes both filter|
pages to the flight controller, stores them there and reads|
them back. If the other page was changed too, it asks first.|
'OK' leaves. With a change not saved it asks 'Save now?':|
'Save' saves, then leaves; 'Discard' leaves without saving.||
LOWPASS FILTER:|
Smooths all above the cutoff; lower is smoother but adds delay.|
Enable off sets its type and cutoff to 0, as the configurator|
does. On again before you leave these pages, it gets them|
back; otherwise it starts at 1st order, 125 Hz.|
Filter type: 1st order or 2nd order. Another type set in the|
configurator stays offered while it is the one set.|
Cutoff frequency (Hz): 0 to 1000. 100 is usual, and|
Rotorflight advises not below 60.||
RPM FILTER:|
The main filter on a helicopter: it follows the head speed and|
removes the rotor and motor vibrations wherever they are.|
It needs a head-speed signal (ESC telemetry or an RPM sensor).|
Strength: Low, Medium or High (more removes more, with more|
delay). Custom keeps a table made in the configurator.|
Minimum frequency (Hz): 1 to 100; 20 is usual. Set it a little|
below the main rotor's frequency at your lowest head speed.||
DYNAMIC FILTER:|
Notches that find the strongest vibrations by themselves.|
Notch count: 0 to 8 (4 to 6 alone, 2 to 4 with the RPM filter).|
Notch Q: how narrow each notch is (1.0 to 10.0).|
2.0 to 4.0 is recommended; below 2.0 adds delay.|
Notch minimum and maximum (Hz): the range they search|
(10 to 200, and 100 to 500). The maximum must be above the|
minimum, or 'Save' refuses.||
RPM AND DYNAMIC FILTER ENABLE:|
These two apply only when the flight controller starts.|
A 'Save' that changes one restarts the flight controller (as|
the configurator does); the page waits for it, then reads back.|
