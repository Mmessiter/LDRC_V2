Batteries: tell the transmitter which batteries you use.|
It needs this for the percentage left and the voltage per cell.|
Touch a choice: it is saved at once.|
'OK' goes back to Transmitter setup.||
'Model battery': 2S to 6S or 12S LiPo, for the model now loaded.|
Its name is at the top left. Each model keeps its own.|
'Transmitter battery': 2 cells, LiFePO4 or LiPo / Li-ion.|
It is the same for every model.||
The warnings are set elsewhere:|
- the transmitter's on Transmitter setup > 'System';|
- the model's on Model setup > 'Options'.||
12S:|
Choose '12S LiPo'.|
A Version 2 receiver needs nothing more.|
It halves any voltage above 25.2 V (a full 6S pack),|
and the transmitter doubles it back.|
A Version 1 receiver needs a voltage divider that halves|
the voltage to its INA219 sensor.|
The sensor cannot cope with more than 26 volts.|
The transmitter does the needed arithmetic.|
