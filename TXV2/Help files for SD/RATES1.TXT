How fast the sticks turn the helicopter, and how much blade|
pitch the collective gives, for the rate shown at the top left.||
TO CHANGE THEM:|
1. Choose the rate with its switch. Wait while it loads.|
2. Touch a box and type the new value.|
3. Press 'Save' (it appears once you touch a box).|
The values are written to the flight controller,|
read back to check, and stored.||
WHICH SWITCH:|
The 'Rates' switch chooses the rate.|
With no 'Rates' switch, it is Rate 4.|
With 'Link rates and banks' on ('Settings ...' on the|
'Rotorflight' page), the bank switch chooses it instead.||
GOOD TO KNOW:|
Save before you move the switch: a move loads the other rate,|
and unsaved changes are lost.|
To check a save, move the switch away and back.|
The page reads the values again.|
If the page says nothing came back, do the same,|
or leave the page and open it again.|
'Save' writes only into the transmitter's rate.|
'Rate 2 / FC 1' at the top left: the flight controller is on|
rate 1. The page shows rate 1, but 'Save' writes nothing, and|
a message says so. Check the set-up of the switch that|
chooses the rate. (The check needs Bluetooth joined.)||
THE NUMBERS:|
Roll, Pitch and Yaw: degrees per second, which the flight|
controller will seek (physics permitting).|
Collective: degrees of blade pitch, if the mechanism allows.|
A value between steps goes to the nearest step|
(163 becomes 160).||
THE COLUMNS:|
They follow the rate's type, on the 'Rates type:' line.|
'6 - ROTORFLIGHT': Rate, Shape and Expo.|
Rate goes in steps of 5, up to 1275 (Collective: 0.125).|
Shape and Expo are whole numbers, 0 to 255.|
Every other type: Centre, Max rate and Expo.|
Centre and Max rate go in steps of 10, up to 2550|
(Collective: 0.25).|
Expo goes from 0 to 1, in steps of 0.01.||
RATES TYPE:|
'Rates type:' names the type the flight controller uses,|
such as '4 - ACTUAL'. To change it:|
1. Touch the type. Turn the wheel to another, press 'OK'.|
2. The rates go to that type's starting values, as in|
Rotorflight's own Configurator: about 240 degrees a second|
at full stick for Roll and Pitch, 400 for Yaw, and 12.5|
degrees of Collective.|
3. Change any value you want, then press 'Save'.|
'Cancel' changes nothing. Nothing is sent before 'Save'.|
'OK' on the type already shown gives its starting values too.|
WHY NOT KEEP THE NUMBERS? Each type reads them its own way.|
Rotorflight's 'Rate 250' is 'Centre 500' to ACTUAL: twice|
as fast, and twice the Collective.||
The numbers here match the Configurator for '4 - ACTUAL',|
and for '6 - ROTORFLIGHT' on 2.3: the easiest to read.|
'6 - ROTORFLIGHT' needs Rotorflight 2.3.||
CURVES:|
'Curves ...' draws this rate's four curves from the numbers|
on the page (typed but not yet saved too), worked out with|
Rotorflight's own formulas. Across: the stick, from full one|
way to full the other. Up: how fast the model turns.|
Roll, Pitch and Yaw share one scale; Collective (degrees of|
blade pitch) has its own. The boxes on the left give each|
one's value at full stick. A curve lying on another (Pitch|
the same as Roll) is drawn dashed, so both show.|
THE DOTS: each curve has a dot where its stick is now, as the|
flight controller reads it. Move a stick: its dot rides along|
its curve, and shows how fast the model would turn there.|
'OK' goes back to the numbers.||
'Dynamics ...' opens 'Rates: dynamics' for the same rate.|
It asks first if you have unsaved changes.|
'OK' goes back to the 'Rotorflight' page.|
With unsaved changes it asks 'Save now?': 'Save' saves them,|
and the page closes once saved; 'Discard' leaves without saving.|
The page opens only with the safety on.|
With no model connected, it shows the model's backup|
(see the help on the 'Rotorflight' page).|
