The flight controller's serial ports (UARTs), and what each|
one does: as on the phone app's 'Ports' page|
(the Configurator's Serial Ports tab).||
Each row is a socket. On a RadioMaster Nexus it shows the name|
printed on the board, then the UART: 'Port A (UART 4)'.|
Port B is UART 6, Port C is UART 3. On the Nexus-X, TELEM is|
UART 2 and S.BUS is UART 1; on the original Nexus, S.BUS is|
UART 2 and DSM is UART 1. Other boards show UART numbers only.||
Tap a row to step its job: 'Nothing', 'ESC telemetry', 'GPS',|
'Dongle on a wire (MSP)' (an LDRC dongle, at 115200),|
'Telemetry out: S.Port', 'Blackbox logger'.|
'As it is' means a job this page does not know.|
A tap takes it to 'Nothing'.|
'Receiver' is the port your receiver is wired to: it cannot be|
changed here. Moved, the transmitter would lose the flight|
controller. To move it, use the phone app's 'Ports' page, with|
a USB-C cable from the receiver to the flight controller.||
'Save' writes all the First-time basics pages, adds the|
features a job needs (ESC telemetry, GPS, telemetry), stores|
them and restarts the flight controller. It asks first.|
A port given a job it is not wired for is simply silent.|
The ports need the model itself: its backup keeps no ports.|
'< Previous' goes back to 'Gear ratio', 'Next >' on to|
'Throttle calibration'.|
'OK' leaves. With a change not saved it asks 'Save now?':|
'Save' saves, then leaves; 'Discard' leaves without saving.|
