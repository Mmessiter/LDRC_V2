Switches and knobs: what each switch or knob of this transmitter|
makes the flight controller do, all on one page.|
A switch job turns something on: arm, rescue, self-level, the|
beeper, the black box and more.|
A selector picks the bank, or the rates, by its position.|
A knob or a switch can change a setting in flight: a gain,|
a rate, the governor and more.||
ROTORFLIGHT'S TWO TABS:|
Rotorflight keeps these on two tabs of its configurator, 'Modes'|
(the switch jobs) and 'Adjustments' (the selectors and knobs),|
and the phone app once did the same. Many found that confusing:|
the bank selector is a switch job to most of us. So this page|
holds them all. It writes each to its own table, out of sight.||
WHAT IT NEEDS:|
The model connected, with the safety on, and a Version 2|
receiver with Bluetooth. With no model, the page uses the|
model's backup on the card, if there is one: a 'Save' then|
changes the backup, offered to the model when it next connects.|
The switch jobs come over the receiver's lead. The selectors and|
knobs also need the USB-C cable from the receiver to the flight|
controller ('USB cable' at the top left): Rotorflight will not|
send them over the lead. Without the cable the page shows the|
switch jobs, says so, and will not add a selector or a knob.||
THE PAGE:|
One job at a time. '<' and '>' beside the title step through them:|
the switch jobs first, then the selectors and knobs.|
A switch job reads 'Does [the job]' and 'Switch on [channel]'.|
A selector or a knob reads 'Changes [the setting]', the bank it|
works in, and 'Knob on [channel]' (or 'Switch, 3 positions',|
'Step adjustment', 'Banks 1 to 6 of this transmitter').|
Tap the job box to choose another job from the list.|
Tap the channel box for the next channel, 6 to 16 (after 16 it|
goes back to 6). Channel 6 is AUX 1 in Rotorflight.|
'Add' opens the list first: choose what the new job does, then|
press 'OK'. It goes on the channel after the one showing.|
'Remove' takes away the job showing, after a question.|
Rotorflight holds 20 switch jobs and 42 adjustments.||
THE LIST ('What it does'):|
The two selectors come first, then the switch jobs, then the|
settings: rates and PIDs, then the rest.|
Turn the list (drag it, or tap a row) until the job is in the|
middle, then press 'OK'. 'Cancel' keeps the old one.|
A switch job can become a setting, and a setting a switch job:|
the old row goes and the new one takes its channel.||
THE BAR:|
The bar is the channel's travel, 875 to 2125 microseconds (us).|
A real switch spans about 988 to 2012.|
The marker line (white, edged in black) is where the channel is|
now: this transmitter's output, as the flight controller sees|
it. The line under the bar says what that gives.|
Switch job: the green zone is where the job is ON. Drag the|
round ends to set it. The line says ON or OFF.|
Selector: one zone per bank. Nothing to drag: the ends are matched|
to this transmitter's banks (below).|
Knob: tap the left half of its zone for the low end value, or|
the right half for the high end value. Drag the round ends to|
set its travel.|
Switch with positions: tap the first or last zone to type its|
value. The zones between are spaced evenly, each showing its|
value. Drag the round ends to the switch's two end positions.|
Step adjustment: tap an outer zone to type the step. Tap the|
grey middle zone's left half for the lowest value, or its right|
half for the highest.||
THE ARM SWITCH:|
The arm switch is this transmitter's safety switch. With a|
Rotorflight model, the safety drives the arming channel (set|
in 'Settings ...').|
So the arm job is kept on that channel: if the flight controller|
had it elsewhere, the page moves it there and says so; 'Save'|
writes it. Change the channel in 'Settings ...'.|
It cannot be swapped for another job, or removed.|
'Save' refuses an ON zone that reaches 988 us: the model would|
arm with the safety on. It also refuses one that stops short of|
2012 us (safety off would not arm), and a set with no arm job.||
THE SELECTORS:|
A 'Bank (Profile) selector' tells Rotorflight which bank the|
switch has chosen; a 'Rates selector' does the same for the|
rates. Without a bank selector, every adjustment works in every|
bank.|
A selector is matched to this transmitter's banks, so there is|
nothing to drag. Its kind box says, for example, 'Banks 1 to 4|
of this transmitter' (1 to 6 with 'Six banks').|
Each bank sends its own value on the bank channel, rising from|
bank 1. The transmitter picks the travel whose even divisions|
give each bank its own zone, with the most room to spare.|
Flip through the banks: the line under the bar shows each bank's|
value, and says so if Rotorflight would pick another bank.|
When the channel does not give every bank a value of its own|
(banks 5 and 6 start as copies of bank 4), or a bank's value|
sits off the middle of its zone, the transmitter sets the|
channel flat in every bank, each in the middle of its zone as|
Rotorflight sees it (988 to 2012 us), and says so. Press 'Save'|
to write the zones to Rotorflight; leave without saving and|
the channel goes back as it was.|
Tap the kind box to match a selector again, or for why it could|
not be matched.||
KIND, BANK AND VALUES:|
Tap the kind box of a setting to step to the next kind:|
Knob: the travel between the round ends sets the value, from the|
low end value to the high end value.|
Switch, 2, 3 or 4 positions: each position sets its own value.|
Step adjustment: a three-position switch. Up adds the step,|
down takes it away, and the centre holds. The value stays|
between the lowest and the highest value.|
The bank box says when a setting works: tap it for 'in bank 1',|
'in bank 2' and so on, then 'in any bank'. It needs a bank|
selector. 'set elsewhere': its condition was made some other|
way, kept as it is until you tap the box.|
A new setting starts at the value it has now, if it can be read.|
A rate (top rotation speed) is in degrees per second, as on the|
Rates page.||
SAVE AND OK:|
'Save' appears once something is changed. It writes the switch|
jobs that changed, then the selectors and knobs that changed,|
stores them, and reads them back to check.|
A note that starts 'Press Save:' means the page has put something|
right: a bank selector that did not follow this transmitter's|
banks; an extra bank selector line (it would fight the first);|
a switch in the old form, one line per position, which|
Rotorflight applies only once per power-up.|
'OK' leaves. With unsaved changes it asks 'Save now?':|
'Save' saves them, and the page closes once saved;|
'Discard' leaves without saving.|
