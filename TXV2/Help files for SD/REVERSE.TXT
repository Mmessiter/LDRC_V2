If a channel moves the wrong way, tick its box to reverse it.|
Nothing changes until you press 'OK'.|
'OK' keeps the changes and goes back to 'Model setup'.||
Reversing comes last: after mixes, trims, subtrim|
and any re-routing on the 'Inputs' page.|
So each box reverses the receiver output of that number.|
With the usual Out numbers on 'Inputs',|
that is the channel named beside the box.||
'Motor off value' ('Model setup', 'Options') is not reversed.|
If you reverse the throttle, change that value to match.|
