LINK RATES AND BANKS:|
On: the bank switch sets the rates too (Bank 2 uses Rate 2).|
Off: the 'Rates' switch sets the rates on its own.|
The switch is saved as soon as you touch it.||
ROTORFLIGHT VERSION:|
The version the receiver reports from the flight controller:|
2.2 or 2.3. It cannot be typed.|
0.0 means no Rotorflight is known.|
The model remembers what the last connection found, and uses|
it until the receiver answers. Once found, it stays for the|
rest of the session, even while the flight controller is|
silent. After 30 seconds connected with no Rotorflight found,|
the model starts at 0.0 next time, until it is found again.|
At 0.0 the arming channel is not used at all: the safety|
switch does not drive it and no gap is left out of the count.|
'Link rates and banks' has no effect either.|
At 2.2 or 2.3 the transmitter's own rate percentages|
are not used: Rotorflight's rates are.|
'Governor ...' needs 2.3.|
The Rates page's columns follow each bank's rates type.||
ARMING CHANNEL:|
The channel the safety switch drives (usually 6), on a|
Rotorflight model only. Safety off arms the flight controller.|
6 to 16: Rotorflight's switches start at channel 6 - or 5,|
when its channel map puts the arm switch there (ExpressLRS).|
Any other entry is ignored: the old channel stays.||
GEAR RATIO (V1 RECEIVER):|
Motor turns for one turn of the main rotor, such as 10.3.|
A Version 1 receiver uses it to work out the head speed.|
A Version 2 receiver does not: Rotorflight sends the head speed.|
With a Version 2 receiver, set Rotorflight's own gear ratio on|
'First-time basics ...' (its 'Gear ratio' page), not here.||
FIRST-TIME BASICS:|
'First-time basics ...' opens four pages for a new model's|
set-up: swash type, which way the rotor turns, tail type, ESC|
signal and brand, motor pole count; 'Gear ratio' (main and|
tail); 'Ports' (what each UART does); 'Throttle calibration'|
(the ESC's throttle range).|
The first two work from the model's backup too; 'Ports' and|
the calibration need the model itself.|
A 'Save' there restarts the flight controller.||
CALIBRATE:|
'Calibrate ...' teaches the flight controller which way is|
level, for self-levelling and rescue. It has its own help.|
