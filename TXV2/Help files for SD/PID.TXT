The flight controller's PID gains for Roll, Pitch and Yaw,|
for the bank shown at the top left.||
TO CHANGE THEM:|
1. Choose the bank with the bank switch. Wait while it loads.|
2. Touch a box and type the new value.|
3. Press 'Save' (it appears once you touch a box).|
The values are written to the flight controller,|
read back to check, and stored.||
GOOD TO KNOW:|
Save before you move the switch: a move loads the other bank,|
and unsaved changes are lost.|
To check a save, move the switch away and back.|
The page reads the values again.|
If the page says nothing came back, do the same,|
or leave the page and open it again.|
Two numbers at the top left, such as 'Bank 3 / FC 2':|
the flight controller is on bank 2, not your switch's bank 3.|
The page shows and saves bank 2.||
THE COLUMNS:|
P: how hard it corrects. Too much gives a fast wobble.|
I: how firmly it holds against drift and wind.|
D: damping, against overshoot.|
FF (feedforward): answers the stick at once.|
Boost: an extra kick on quick stick moves.|
HSI offset: Roll and Pitch only.|
In the Configurator, FF is F, Boost is B and HSI offset is O.||
'Advanced ...' opens 'PID +' for the same bank.|
'OK' goes back to the Rotorflight options.|
Both ask first if you have unsaved changes.|
The page opens only with the safety on.|
With no model connected, it shows the model's backup|
(see the Rotorflight options help).|
