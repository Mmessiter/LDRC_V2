Copy the tuning of one bank into another, so you can tune one bank|
and give it to the others.||
It needs the model connected, with the safety on,|
and a Version 2 receiver with Bluetooth.|
With no model, the copy is made in the backup on the card.|
It is offered to the model as edits when it next connects.||
HOW:|
1. Tap the box beside 'From' for the bank to copy,|
and the box beside 'To' for the bank to copy into.|
Each tap steps to the next bank.|
'From' and 'To' are never the same bank.|
2. Switch on what to copy (green = on).|
3. Press 'Copy bank 1 to bank 2' (it names your banks),|
then 'OK' to the question.|
Do NOT move the bank switch while it runs.||
WHAT TO COPY:|
PIDs, PID+ and rescue: the whole flight tuning.|
Rates: the rates of the 'From' bank, into the 'To' bank.|
Governor gains: the governor settings, but not the head speed.|
Head speed too: the head speed as well.|
Leave it OFF normally: each bank keeps its own head speed, which|
is usually the whole point of having banks.|
Switching it on switches on 'Governor gains'. Switching|
'Governor gains' off switches it off.|
Whatever is off stays as it was in the target bank.||
SCALE GAINS TO RPM:|
Switch it on when the two banks fly at different head speeds.|
Why: the rotor's control power grows with head speed squared.|
A tune made at one head speed is too sharp at a faster one,|
and too soft at a slower one.|
With this on, the copied P, I, D, F and B gains are scaled by the|
two banks' head speeds:|
Into a faster bank: down by the speed ratio to the power 1.5.|
Into a slower bank: up by the plain speed ratio, at most 25 %.|
It works only with 'PIDs, PID+ and rescue' on.|
With 'Head speed too' on, both banks run one speed: no change.|
If a bank has no head speed set, the gains are copied unchanged.||
WHAT HAPPENS:|
The copy is Rotorflight's own, as on the receiver's own page.|
It brings the source's governor along,|
so the target's governor is put right afterwards.|
Everything is stored, then the target is read back and compared.|
Then the flight controller goes back to the transmitter's banks.|
The answer lists what was copied, and any change to the gains.|
It also says if the target read back the same.|
If it did not, check that bank.|
'OK' leaves (not while a copy runs).|
