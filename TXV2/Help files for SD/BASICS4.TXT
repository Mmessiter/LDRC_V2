Teaches the ESC the throttle range Rotorflight sends|
(1000 to 2000 us): the configurator's Motors tab procedure,|
with no computer. The phone app has the same on its ESC page.||
BEFORE YOU START:|
Blades off, or the pinion out of the main gear.|
Flight pack unplugged. The flight controller powered by USB|
(a power bank), so that the battery can be plugged in AFTER|
full throttle is set.|
Safety on, and nobody at the sticks.||
HOW IT GOES:|
1. Tap 'Full throttle' and answer the question.|
   The top line says FULL THROTTLE held.|
2. Plug the flight pack in. The ESC sees full throttle as it|
   wakes, and beeps to say it has learned the top.|
3. Tap 'Minimum'. The hold is let go, the ESC sees minimum|
   and beeps again: the bottom is learned.|
4. 'Calibrated: unplug the battery'. Done.||
HOW IT IS KEPT SAFE:|
The flight controller obeys the hold only while disarmed, and|
forgets it by itself one second after the last renewal. This|
page renews it about three times a second while the top line|
says held. Leave the page, lose the link or flick the safety,|
and the hold is gone a second later. If a renewal gets no|
answer in time, the page says the hold lapsed: start again.||
'< Previous' goes back to the ports. 'OK' leaves.|
Nothing here is saved: the ESC itself remembers the range.|
