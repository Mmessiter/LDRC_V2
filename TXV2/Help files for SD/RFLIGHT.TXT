View and change the setup of a flight controller that runs|
Rotorflight (2.2 or later), at the field, with no laptop.||
ANIMATION: 'Animation', top left, tours this menu. Each page's|
own help has its own animation too.||
A NEW HELICOPTER:|
Set it up with the phone app: 'Rotorflight', 'Wizards',|
'New helicopter' takes you through every step.|
Or do the basics here: 'Settings ...', then|
'First-time basics ...'.|
Only putting Rotorflight onto the flight controller needs a|
computer, with Rotorflight's Configurator.||
BEFORE YOU START:|
Land, put the safety on and switch the motor off.|
If the safety is off or the motor is on, this page warns you.|
The pages that change the flight controller will not work|
while the model could be armed.|
The model needs a Version 2 receiver: these pages use Bluetooth.|
Back up each setup you are happy with: 'Backup & restore ...'.||
HOW THE PAGES WORK:|
Each page reads the flight controller's values as it opens.|
Touch a box, type the new value, then press 'Save'.|
The values are written to the flight controller,|
read back to check, and stored.|
'OK' with edits not saved asks 'Save now?'.|
'Save' saves them, then the page closes.|
'Discard' leaves without saving.|
If the save is refused or fails, your edits stay on the page.|
Every page has its own 'Help'.||
BANKS:|
The top left shows the bank and the rate in use.|
The bank switch chooses the bank for 'PIDs ...',|
'Governor ...' and 'Rescue ...'.|
The 'Rates' switch chooses the rate for 'Rates ...'.|
With 'Link rates and banks' on ('Settings ...'),|
the bank switch chooses the rate too.|
'PID + ...' and 'Dynamics ...' follow their main page's switch.|
A 'Save' on the PIDs, rates or governor pages goes only into the|
transmitter's bank. If the flight controller is on another bank|
('Bank 2 / FC 1'), nothing is written: check the switch set-up.||
THE BUTTONS:|
'Easy tuning ...': the phone app's sliders - start, agility,|
stick feel, and softer or sharper - for tuning at the field.|
'PIDs ...' and 'Rates ...': the tuning of each bank.|
Each has a second page: 'PID + ...' and 'Dynamics ...'.|
'Governor ...': the head speed and the governor.|
It needs Rotorflight 2.3.|
'Rescue ...': what happens when you flick the rescue switch.|
'Servos ...' and 'Travel extents ...': the servos, and how far|
the swash and tail move, with a bench measure for a pitch gauge.|
'Filters ...': gyro smoothing and the vibration notches.|
'Adjustments ...': a knob or switch changes a setting in flight.|
It needs the USB cable between receiver and flight controller.|
'Switches ...': what each switch makes the flight controller do.|
'Copy a bank ...': copies the tuning of one bank into another.|
'Battery ...' and 'Black box ...': the flight pack and the|
flight recorder.|
'Settings ...': 'Link rates and banks', the Rotorflight version,|
the arming channel and 'Gear ratio (V1 receiver)'; and|
'First-time basics ...': swash type, rotor direction, tail type,|
ESC signal and brand, motor poles, gear ratios, the ports and|
the ESC throttle calibration (a new model's whole set-up);|
and 'Calibrate ...': the level.|
'OK' goes back to the front page.||
THE ARMING LINE:|
The line under the buttons says whether the model will arm:|
'Ready to arm' (green), 'Armed' (red), or 'Will not arm:'|
and the main reason (yellow), such as 'Throttle is not at idle'.|
Touch the line to list the reasons (up to four).|
It shows only with the model connected and Bluetooth joined.||
BLUETOOTH:|
The box left of 'OK' shows the link to the receiver.|
A blue tooth: joined.|
'Connecting Bluetooth: wait': joining.|
'Bluetooth: not joined': it could not join. It tries again.|
'No Bluetooth': no receiver that can carry it.|
It needs a Version 2 receiver (0.9.874 or later),|
switched on and in reach.|
Bluetooth is off while the safety is off|
(with no safety switch: while the motor is on).|
If it keeps connecting, close the phone app if it is open:|
the receiver takes one Bluetooth connection at a time.||
COMMAND LINE:|
'Command line ...' opens Rotorflight's own command line (CLI).|
It needs Bluetooth joined, and the USB cable|
between the receiver and the flight controller.|
Touch the line at the bottom to type a command.|
What the flight controller prints scrolls in the box:|
drag it with a finger.|
Type save to keep what you set, or exit to leave without saving.|
Either one restarts the flight controller.|
'OK' leaves too, and restarts it once a command was typed.|
If you changed something and did not type save, it asks first.|
The model cannot arm while the command line is open.||
BACKUP AND RESTORE:|
'Backup & restore ...' keeps the model's Rotorflight tune and|
set-up (not ports, LED, GPS or ESC) on the transmitter's card,|
and puts it back.|
It can also keep the settings as text ('Diff to card')|
and replay them ('Execute diff').||
NO MODEL CONNECTED:|
The pages show and edit the model's backup on the card.|
The box left of 'OK' then says 'From the backup file'.|
Your edits wait in the backup.|
When the model is next connected and you open this page,|
it asks once to write them to the model.|
To send them later: 'Send to model' on 'Backup & restore ...'.|
With no backup, 'PIDs ...', 'Rates ...' and 'Governor ...'|
show an older copy kept in the model's file.|
A 'Save' there changes only that copy.|
It is not offered to the model when it is next connected.|
Most other pages need the model, or its backup.|
'Easy tuning ...' needs the model: its sliders start from the|
model's own numbers, measured from the backup.|
'Calibrate level', the black box's memory and the arming line|
always need the model itself.|
