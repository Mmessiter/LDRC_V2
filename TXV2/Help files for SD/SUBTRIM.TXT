Subtrim sets the exact centre of each servo for this model.||
1. Touch a channel to choose it. Its box turns white.|
2. Move the slider, or press '-' and '+'.|
The servo moves as you go.|
'Zero' puts the chosen channel back to 0.|
'OK' keeps the changes and goes back to 'Model setup'.||
Subtrim is the same in every bank.||
Very large subtrims should not be needed. If one is,|
change the linkage so that a small amount is enough.||
Subtrim always uses a multiplier of 5.|
So changing the 'Trim multiplier' does not change any subtrim.|
