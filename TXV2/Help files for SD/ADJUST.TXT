Adjustments: a knob or a switch on this transmitter changes a Rotorflight setting in flight (the configurator's Adjustments tab). Needs the model connected, the safety on, and the USB-C cable from the receiver to the flight controller: Rotorflight cannot send its adjustments over the receiver's lead.||

One adjustment at a time: < Prev and Next > move between them, Add makes a new one after the one showing, Remove takes it away. Setting: < and > step through what can be adjusted, rates and PIDs first. Channel: the knob or switch, by this transmitter's channel number and name (channel 6 is AUX1).||

Kind, tap to change: Knob (the whole travel maps the low end value to the high end value), Switch with 2 or 3 positions (one value per position), Step up / down (a three-position switch: up adds the step, down takes it, within the lowest and highest values).||

In bank: Any, or one bank only. Needs a 'PID bank switch (1 to 6)' adjustment first, with one value per position: that is how Rotorflight knows which bank the switch means.||

Now shows the setting's present value, read from the flight controller when the adjustment is added or its setting changed (in the bank in use only). Channel now is this transmitter's live output for the channel: move the knob or switch to see where its positions fall (875 to 2125).||

Save writes all 42 of Rotorflight's lines, stores them, and reads them back. A rate (top rotation speed) is shown in degrees per second as on the Rates page.||

OK leaves; edits not saved are discarded after a question.||
