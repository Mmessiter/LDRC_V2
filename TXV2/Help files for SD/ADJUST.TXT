Make a knob or a switch on this transmitter change a Rotorflight|
setting in flight. Each adjustment is one setting, worked by|
one channel.|
The phone app has the same on its 'In-flight adjustments'|
page (the Configurator: its Adjustments tab).||
It needs the model connected, with the safety on,|
and a Version 2 receiver with Bluetooth.|
It also needs the USB-C cable from the receiver to the flight|
controller ('USB cable' at the top left).|
Rotorflight cannot send adjustments over the receiver's lead.|
Without the cable, the page says so and goes back.|
With no model, the page shows the backup on the card,|
if the backup was made with the cable in.|
'Save' then changes the backup.|
The change is offered to the model when it next connects.||
THE PAGE:|
'<' and '>' beside the title step through the adjustments.|
'Add' makes a new one after the one showing: a Knob for|
'Pitch P gain', on the same channel. Rotorflight holds up to 42.|
'Remove' deletes the one showing, after a question.||
THE SETTING AND THE BANK:|
Tap the setting box beside 'Changes' to open the list,|
'What it changes'.|
Rates and PIDs come first.|
Turn the list (drag it, or tap a row) until the setting is in the|
middle, then press 'OK'. 'Cancel' keeps the old one.|
A new setting starts at the value it has now, if it can be read.|
A rate (top rotation speed) is in degrees per second,|
as on the Rates page.|
The box on the right says when it works: tap it for 'in bank 1',|
'in bank 2' and so on, then 'in any bank'.|
'set elsewhere': its condition was made some other way.|
It is kept as it is until you tap the box.|
The bank box needs a 'Bank (Profile) selector' adjustment:|
it tells Rotorflight which bank the switch has chosen.|
Without one, every adjustment works in every bank.|
The list here does not offer one. If the model has none,|
choose its 'Bank (Profile)' channel on the phone app's|
'Transmitter channels' page.|
A bank selector is the bank itself, so it has no bank box.||
KIND AND CHANNEL:|
Tap the box under 'Changes' to step to the next kind:|
Knob: the travel between the round ends sets the value, from the|
low end value to the high end value.|
Switch, 2, 3 or 4 positions: each position sets its own value.|
Step adjustment: a three-position switch. Up adds the step,|
down takes it away, and the centre holds. The value stays|
between the lowest and the highest value.|
Tap the channel box for the next channel, 6 to 16.|
(Channel 6 is AUX1 in Rotorflight.)||
THE BAR:|
The bar is the channel's travel, 875 to 2125 microseconds (us).|
A real switch spans about 988 to 2012.|
The marker line (white, edged in black) shows where the|
channel is now: this transmitter's output, as the flight|
controller sees it.|
The line under the bar says what that gives.|
Knob: tap the left half of its zone for the low end value,|
or the right half for the high end value.|
Drag the round ends to set its travel.|
Switch: tap the first or last zone to type its value.|
The zones between are spaced evenly, and each shows its value.|
Drag the round ends to the switch's two end positions.|
Step adjustment: tap an outer zone to type the step.|
Tap the grey middle zone's left half for the lowest value,|
or its right half for the highest.|
Drag the round dividers to where up and down start.|
A switch's last value must be above its first.|
A knob's high end value must be above its low end value.|
'Save' refuses otherwise.|
If your switch or knob works the other way round,|
reverse the channel.||
THE BANK SELECTOR:|
A bank selector ('Bank (Profile) selector' or 'Rates selector')|
is matched to this transmitter's banks, so there is nothing|
to drag. Its kind box then says, for example,|
'Banks 1 to 4 of this transmitter'.|
Each bank sends its own value on the bank channel.|
The values rise from bank 1.|
The transmitter picks the travel whose even divisions give|
each bank its own zone, with the most room to spare.|
A bank selector that already does that is left alone.|
Flip through the banks: the line under the bar shows each bank's|
value, and says so if Rotorflight would pick another bank.|
When no travel can tell the banks apart, the hint says so.|
Tap the kind box for why. Then space the banks' values on that|
channel more evenly in Model setup.||
SAVE AND OK:|
'Save' appears once something is changed.|
It writes only the lines that changed, stores them,|
and reads them all back to check.|
A note that starts 'Press Save:' means the page has put something|
right. Press 'Save' to keep it. This happens for:|
a bank selector that did not follow this transmitter's banks;|
an extra bank selector line (it would fight the first);|
a switch in the old form, one line per position.|
Rotorflight applies those only once per power-up.|
A switch is now one line, its travel mapped onto the values,|
so it works at every change.||
'OK' leaves. With unsaved changes it asks 'Save now?':|
'Save' saves them, and the page closes once saved;|
'Discard' leaves without saving.|
A 'Press Save:' note after that save keeps the page open.|
