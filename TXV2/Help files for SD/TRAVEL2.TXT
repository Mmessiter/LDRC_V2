ROLL AND PITCH TOGETHER:|
Cyclic gain and Cyclic pitch limit are each one box for both|
roll and pitch. Type a new value and both take it.|
Left alone, each keeps its own (the phone app sets a roll and|
a pitch gain). Where they differ, this page shows roll's gain|
and pitch's limit.||
GAINS:|
Cyclic gain and Collective gain (%): how much of a full stick|
reaches the swash (0 to 1000, one decimal).|
Up/down balance (%): evens out the collective, so up and down|
give the same pitch (-25 to 25, one decimal). Rotorflight calls|
it geo correction (not the servos' Geometry correction).||
LIMITS:|
Cyclic pitch limit and Collective pitch limit (deg): the most|
blade pitch each may give, either way (0 to 30, one decimal).|
Total pitch limit (deg): cyclic and collective together|
(0 to 36; 0 = off). When on, Rotorflight keeps it at least|
6 degrees above the collective limit: a 'Save' may raise it.||
SWASH:|
Swash phase angle (deg): turns the cyclic a little round the|
mast (-180 to 180, one decimal).|
Tilt correction + and - (%): for a swash that tilts as the|
collective moves (-100 to 100, whole numbers).|
