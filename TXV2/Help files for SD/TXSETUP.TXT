Transmitter setup: the settings for the transmitter itself.|
Most are the same for every model.|
A model's own settings are on Model setup.||
THE BUTTONS:|
'Calibrate': set up the sticks, switches and trims.|
Do this first, and again after new gimbals or a new stick mode.|
It will not open while a model is connected.|
'System': general settings and binding, the clock, advanced.|
'Sounds etc.': voices, sounds, vario, brightness and volume.|
'Switches': the job of each switch.|
'Appearance': background picture, themes, your own front screen.|
A theme is a panel colour with its text colour.|
Its 'Help' says more.|
'Batteries': model battery cells, transmitter battery type.|
'Scan RF': the Interference scan (see SCAN RF below).|
'Buddy': instructor and pupil fly one model, a transmitter each.|
'Raining': Pong, a game for a day too wet to fly.|
'WiFi': choose your network and type its password, once.|
At the field, your phone's hotspot will do.|
'OK': back to the front screen.||
SCAN RF:|
The Interference scan shows how busy each 2.4 GHz channel is.|
The link to the model stops while the scan runs.|
With a model connected you are asked first.|
Never scan while the model flies.|
Each yellow bar is one channel, 1 MHz wide.|
The busiest channel fills the graph; the others are to scale.|
The red line is the top of the legal band (2.4835 GHz).|
Channels to its right are outside it.|
A hit is a signal heard on a channel.|
'Average hits per second' counts the whole band.|
Lower is quieter.|
'Quietest channel' and 'Noisiest channel' look only|
inside the legal band.|
Each shows the channel number and its hits since the scan began.|
Channel numbers count MHz from 2.400 GHz:|
channel 12 is 2.412 GHz.|
'Restart' clears the graph and starts again.|
'OK': back to Transmitter setup, and the radio back to normal.|
How slowly the bars fall back: 'Radio scanner sensitivity'|
on the Advanced page of 'System'.||
TRANSMITTER UPDATES:|
'Transmitter updates' asks messiter.com for the latest version.|
Nothing is installed unless you press 'Install now'.|
Your models, settings and logs are copied|
to the screen's card first.|
'Earlier versions' puts a previous version back.|
It updates the transmitter and its screen only.|
The button is greyed out while a model is connected.|
Switch the model off and wait a minute.|
At power-up on a WiFi it knows, with no model connected, the|
transmitter looks for a newer version by itself. If there is|
one, it says so along the top of the screen for a few seconds.|
Nothing is installed until you press 'Install now' here.|
The safety must be on (with no safety switch: the motor off).|
Firmware that reads a model's settings differently is not kept:|
the old firmware comes back by itself, and the screen says so.|
The receiver's own update is 'Receiver updates' on Model setup.|
It works while a model is connected.||
WIFI AND BLUETOOTH:|
They switch themselves off while the safety is off.|
(With no safety switch: while the motor is on.)||
WORKSHOP DOOR:|
'Workshop door' lets workshop tools reach the screen over WiFi.|
Join a WiFi network first: the door opens on that network only.|
Press it again to shut the door.|
Shut, the screen answers nothing but its status.|
