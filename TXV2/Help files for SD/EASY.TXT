The phone app's 'Easy tuning' sliders, on the transmitter.|
Slide, then press 'Save'. Each slider moves the real numbers|
on its own page (Governor, Rates or PIDs). Nothing is written|
until you press 'Save'.||
FILMS: 'Films', top left of every help page, has a short film|
of Easy tuning (with the safety on). Tap it to pause it, then|
slide the bar to any moment.||
It needs the model connected, the safety on, and a Version 2|
receiver with Bluetooth. It tunes the bank your bank switch is|
on, and the rates your rates switch is on, as the other pages|
do. If the flight controller is on another bank, it says so.||
THE NUMBERS ABOVE THE SLIDERS:|
While your finger is on a slider, a box above the sliders|
shows the numbers that slider moves: what the model has now|
-> what 'Save' would write. One number: 'Save' leaves it.|
The box goes when you lift your finger.||
THE CENTRE:|
Each slider's centre is the model's numbers at your last|
backup ('Backup & restore ...' on the Rotorflight page).|
After a save a slider stays where you left it, so it shows|
how far you have come. Back up, and every slider re-centres|
on that day's numbers.|
With no backup, the centre is the numbers the page opened with.|
A slider at the end of its travel says so when you let it go:|
a backup re-centres it.||
START (the same for every bank):|
Moves three numbers on the Governor page and nothing else:|
spool-up time, startup time and handover throttle.|
Full left doubles the two times and lowers the handover 15 %.|
Full right halves the times and raises the handover 15 %.|
Saving it restarts the flight controller: blades off.||
AGILITY AND STICK FEEL (this rate):|
Agility: how fast roll, pitch and yaw turn at full stick,|
from half to double the centre.|
Stick feel: the expo, from 35 softer to 35 more direct.|
They work with the Rotorflight, Actual and Quickrates rates|
types. With another type these rows are hidden: use|
'Rates ...' instead.||
CYCLIC AND TAIL (this bank):|
Softer or sharper: P, I, D and F of roll and pitch (Cyclic),|
or of yaw (Tail), times 0.7 to 1.3.|
Boost and the HSI offsets are left alone.|
Wobbles or shakes: softer.|
Sluggish, drifting or mushy: sharper.|
Small steps, one flight each.||
PID SPOOL-UP (the switch at the bottom):|
A Rotorflight governor setting, one in each bank.|
Off (Rotorflight's own default): as the head spools up, the|
flight controller raises the throttle at a steady rate. The|
governor takes over only when the head is almost at speed.|
On: once the throttle passes the handover point, the governor|
takes over at once. It raises the HEAD SPEED at a steady rate,|
right up to your head speed: a smoother spool-up that ends|
exactly on speed.|
With the switch on, saving a Start change also turns PID|
spool-up on in every bank. To do only that, switch it off and|
on again, then press 'Save'. It never turns PID spool-up off|
(the phone app can, bank by bank).||
SAVE AND OK:|
'Save' writes only what you moved, stores it, reads it back.|
It asks first, and says what it saved.|
'OK' leaves. With changes not saved it asks 'Save now?':|
'Save' saves, then leaves. 'Discard' leaves without saving.|
