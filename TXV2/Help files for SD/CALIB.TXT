Calibration teaches the transmitter where its sticks and switches|
end and centre, and which way its trims work.|
Do it before you set up and fly: it is critically important!|
Do it again after new gimbals or a change of stick mode.|
New transmitter, or a new owner?|
Start with 'Reset transmitter' (see RESET TRANSMITTER below).|
The page will not open while a model is connected.|
Do not switch the transmitter off while calibrating!||
STICKS AND SWITCHES:|
Calibrate channels 1 to 8, the top edge switches and the trims.|
Nothing else needs it.|
Channels 1 to 4 are the two sticks;|
channels 5 to 8 are the four front switches or knobs.|
1. Press 'Calibrate sticks etc.'|
2. Move both sticks, and the front switches or knobs, to all|
their extreme positions several times.|
Then press 'Next'.|
3. Centre the sticks (the throttle stick too)|
and the front switches or knobs.|
Push all four top edge switches AWAY from you,|
not in the middle.|
The box says back or forward:|
always use away, so each switch works the right way round.|
Then press 'Finish'.|
This records the centres, and which way each top switch works.|
The box then says 'Calibration saved.'|
and the button 'Calibrate again'.|
If a stick is centred but its channel's output is not,|
the box names it: 'Aileron is 1456 at centre: its curve,|
trim or subtrim moves it.' That is not the calibration:|
look at that channel's curve, trim and subtrim.|
Once you have started, go on to 'Finish' before you press 'OK'.|
'OK' part way through keeps the old calibration, and says so.|
The bars 'Ch 1' to 'Ch 8' grow from the middle|
as you move each control.||
TRIMS:|
'Calibrate trims ...' opens 'Set trim directions'.|
Push each trim button the way the page asks,|
one at a time, in any order:|
aileron RIGHT, elevator DOWN, throttle DOWN, rudder RIGHT.|
Each line then says '... trim: done'.|
'OK' goes back to Calibration.|
If you skip this, a trim might work backwards!||
RESET TRANSMITTER:|
For a new transmitter or a new owner. You are asked first.|
It takes a few minutes.|
It deletes all model memories (but NOT their backup files).|
It puts most transmitter settings back to their defaults.|
The clock goes back to the date this firmware was built.|
Set it again on the Clock page: 'System', then 'Next >'.|
Or let it set itself over WiFi.|
The reset sets stick mode 2.|
Fly mode 1? Set 'Stick mode' in 'System' BEFORE you calibrate.|
At power-up, and when a model is chosen, a Rotorflight model is checked: aileron, elevator and rudder must give 1500 with the sticks centred, in every bank.|
A bank that does not is named, with the channel and its value: look at that bank's curve, trim or subtrim.|
