How the model follows the stick, for the rate shown at the|
top left. These are the advanced part of the Rates tab|
in Rotorflight's Configurator.||
TO CHANGE THEM:|
1. Choose the rate with its switch, as on the Rates page.|
Wait while it loads.|
2. Touch a box and type the new value.|
3. Press 'Save' (it appears once you touch a box).|
The values are written to the flight controller,|
read back to check, and stored.||
GOOD TO KNOW:|
Save before you move the switch: a move loads the other rate,|
and unsaved changes are lost.|
To check a save, move the switch away and back.|
The page reads the values again.|
If the page says nothing came back, do the same,|
or leave the page and open it again.|
'Rate 2 / FC 1' at the top left: the flight controller|
is on rate 1, so the page shows and saves rate 1.||
FOR ROLL, PITCH, YAW AND COLLECTIVE:|
Response time [ms]: how long the model takes to reach the rate|
the stick asks for. Higher feels softer. 0 is off.|
Setpoint boost gain: extra response on quick stick moves.|
Setpoint boost cutoff [Hz]: the filter on that boost.|
Lower is smoother.||
FOR YAW ONLY:|
Dynamic ceiling gain, Dynamic deadband gain and|
Dynamic deadband filter [Hz], as in the Configurator.||
THE NUMBERS:|
Dynamic deadband filter has one decimal place, such as 6.0|
(up to 25.5). All the others are whole numbers, 0 to 255.||
'OK' leaves the page. It asks first if you have unsaved changes.|
With no model connected the page shows the model's backup|
(see the Rotorflight options help).|
