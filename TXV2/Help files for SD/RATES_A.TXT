How the model follows the stick, for the rate shown at the|
top left. The phone app has these in the 'Dynamics' card|
of its 'Rates' page (in the Configurator: the Rates tab).||
TO CHANGE THEM:|
1. Choose the rate with its switch, as on the Rates page.|
Wait while it loads.|
2. Touch a box and type the new value.|
3. Press 'Save' (it appears once you touch a box).|
The values are written to the flight controller,|
read back to check, and stored.||
GOOD TO KNOW:|
Save before you move the switch: a move loads the other rate,|
and unsaved changes are lost.|
To check a save, move the switch away and back.|
The page reads the values again.|
If the page says nothing came back, do the same,|
or leave the page and open it again.|
'Save' writes only into the transmitter's rate.|
'Rate 2 / FC 1' at the top left: the flight controller is on|
rate 1. The page shows rate 1, but 'Save' writes nothing, and|
a message says so. Check the set-up of the switch that|
chooses the rate. (The check needs Bluetooth joined.)||
FOR ROLL, PITCH, YAW AND COLLECTIVE:|
Response time (ms): how long the model takes to reach the rate|
the stick asks for. Higher feels softer. 0 is off.|
Stick sharpening (boost gain): extra response on quick stick|
moves (Configurator: setpoint boost gain).|
Sharpening smoothing (Hz): the filter on that boost. Lower is|
smoother (Configurator: setpoint boost cutoff).||
FOR YAW ONLY:|
Fast-move headroom (Configurator: dynamic ceiling gain).|
Stick-centre quieting (Configurator: dynamic deadband gain).|
Quieting smoothing (Hz): the filter on the quieting|
(Configurator: dynamic deadband filter).||
THE NUMBERS:|
Quieting smoothing has one decimal place, such as 6.0|
(up to 25.5). All the others are whole numbers, 0 to 255.||
'OK' leaves the page. With unsaved changes it asks 'Save now?':|
'Save' saves them, and the page closes once saved;|
'Discard' leaves without saving.|
With no model connected the page shows the model's backup|
(see the help on the 'Rotorflight' page).|
