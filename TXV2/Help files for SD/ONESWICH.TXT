Choose this switch's job, then press 'OK' to save it.|
One job per switch: giving a job to this switch takes it from|
any switch that had it.|
The jobs belong to the transmitter, so every model uses|
the same switches.||
THE JOBS:|
A switch's positions are near (towards you), mid and away.|
'Banks 1 2 3': away is bank 1, mid is bank 2, near is bank 3.|
'Bank 4 & Motor': away is motor on, in the bank the 'Banks 1 2 3'|
switch picks. Mid is bank 4 with the motor on. Near is bank 4|
with the motor off.|
With 'Six banks' on ('Model options'), mid and near pick bank 4, 5|
or 6 by the 'Banks 1 2 3' switch: away is 4, mid 5, near 6.|
So the two switches together reach all six banks.|
'Safety': near is safety off. With a Rotorflight model, safety|
off arms the model.|
'Rates': away, mid and near pick rates 1, 2 and 3.|
'Buddy': hands control to the buddy.|
A spring-return switch is best for 'Buddy'.|
What each 'Buddy' position does is set on 'Transmitter setup',|
then 'Buddy'. There, 'Down', 'Middle' and 'Up' mean|
away, mid and near.|
'Channel 5' to 'Channel 16' (by name if you renamed the channel):|
the switch becomes that channel's input.|
Any channel can then use it on the 'Inputs' page.|
Given channel 5 to 8, the switch takes the place of the front|
switch or knob there.|
'Not used': no job.||
'Reversed' turns the switch round, so that near and away swap.||
SWITCHES 5 TO 8 (ON THE FRONT):|
They are the inputs of channels 5 to 8 (a knob there|
reads as a switch).|
With no other job, each one is marked with its own channel,|
'Channel 5' to 'Channel 8'.|
Any other job takes the switch away from its channel, whose input|
then sits at its centre.|
'Not used' does the same and nothing else.|
