How the yaw stick reaches the tail:|
the configurator's Tail rotor settings section.||
Tap a number to type a new one.|
Tap the switch to change it (green = on).|
'Save' appears once something is changed: it writes all the|
Mixer pages, after a question.|
'< Previous' goes back to main rotor geometry, 'Next >' on to|
the bench measure (a pitch gauge check), changes kept.|
'OK' leaves; if a change was not saved, it asks first.||
Tail rotor type shows what the flight controller has (Variable|
pitch, Motorised or Bi-directional). It is set in the|
configurator, and the rows below change with it.|
Yaw reversed: green = reversed (the configurator's Yaw control|
direction). Check on the bench which way the tail moves.|
Yaw calibration [%]: how much of a full stick reaches the tail|
(0 to 1000, one decimal).||
VARIABLE PITCH TAIL:|
Yaw centre trim [deg]: the blade angle with the yaw stick|
centred (-24 to 24, one decimal).|
Yaw limit CW and CCW [deg]: most blade angle each way (0 to 60).||
MOTORISED OR BI-DIRECTIONAL TAIL:|
Yaw centre offset [%]: the output with the yaw stick centred|
(-100 to 100, one decimal).|
Yaw limit CW and CCW [%]: most motor output each way (0 to 250).|
Motor idle throttle [%]: the tail motor's idle (0 to 25).|
It shows only for these tails.|
