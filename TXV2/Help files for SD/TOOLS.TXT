Five helpers that make a Rotorflight setup simpler.|
16 MB screens only.||
NEW HELICOPTER GUIDE:|
Every setup step in order, over the pages you already know.|
A step opens its page; OK on that page brings you back and|
ticks the step. The ticks are kept for each model.||
CHECK MY SETUP:|
Reads the flight controller and lists anything unfinished|
or odd, each with a button to the page that puts it right.||
DIRECTION CHECK:|
Tilt and turn the model: it says whether the flight|
controller sees each move the right way round.||
WHAT CHANGED?:|
Compares the flight controller with this model's backup on|
the card, setting by setting. 'Put back' returns one setting|
to the backup's value.||
UNDO THE LAST SAVE:|
Puts back what the last Rotorflight save replaced - the line|
under the button says which save. Switches & knobs, Copy a|
bank, the ports and a level calibration cannot be undone.||
COMMAND LINE:|
Rotorflight's own command line (CLI): see 'COMMAND LINE' in|
the help of the 'Rotorflight' page.||
All of them need the model connected and the safety on.|
