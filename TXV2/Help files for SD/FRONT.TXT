The front screen: keep it, or your own front screen, in view|
while you fly. It shows the bank, rate, time, trims,|
motor timer and batteries.||
FILMS: every help page has 'Films' at its top left: short|
films of how to do things, with sound. Safety on (or the|
motor off, if no safety switch is set up).|
Tap a film to pause it. Slide the bar to any moment.|
'Play' carries on, 'Films' goes back to the list and 'Exit'|
leaves the films.||
'Transmitter' and 'Model' (bottom corners) open the setups.||
'Defined screens' (bottom middle) shows your own front screen|
instead, with your choice of numbers, big and clear.|
Its 'Original screen' button brings this screen back.|
Your choice stays through take-offs, landings and switching off,|
until you press the other button. Design your own screens in|
Transmitter setup / 'Appearance' / 'Defined front screens'.||
Touch the model's picture to choose another picture.|
With Rotorflight, touch the RPM box to open the 'Rotorflight'|
page (put the safety on and the motor off first).||
With a model connected, switching the motor on brings the front|
screen back from any other page. Not while a question is|
waiting, or you are a buddy pupil, or during a calibration,|
a scan, or a Rotorflight backup or restore.||
LEFT COLUMN:|
At the top, your name ('Owner's name', Transmitter setup /|
'System'). Under it, notes:|
AMS is on (or off): when a receiver connects, the transmitter|
finds its model by itself ('Automatic model selection (AMS)',|
Transmitter setup / 'System'). Shown until a model connects.|
'Trims: all banks': a trim change goes to every bank|
('Copy trims to all banks', Model setup / 'Options').|
With Rotorflight: head speed (RPM), current (Amps), capacity|
used (mAh) and the ESC temperature (ESC).|
Battery LOW: see BATTERIES below.|
MOTOR IS ON! (only with 'Use motor kill switch' on): the motor|
switch was on at switch-on, or when the link was lost.|
Nothing is sent to the model until you switch the motor off|
and press 'OK' on the message 'The motor switch is ON.'||
MODEL NAME (under the picture):|
The number after the name is the model's number, as in (3).|
Square brackets, as in [3], or [AMS is on] on the left, mean|
this model has no stored ID yet ('Model IDs', Model setup).||
CHANNEL BARS (top right):|
Channels 1 to 16, in groups of four. Each bar grows from the|
middle line: to the left below centre, to the right above.|
Under the bars, the Motor box. With 'Use motor kill switch' on|
(Model setup / 'Options'), it is red while the safety is on,|
and green while it is off.||
MOTOR TIMER (the big numbers):|
It runs while the motor is on, and pauses while it is off.|
It starts again each time a model connects.|
With 'Timer voice' on (Transmitter setup / 'Sounds etc.'), each|
whole minute up to ten is spoken.|
To count down instead (Model setup / 'Options'): turn on|
'Timer counts down' and set 'Count down from (minutes)'.|
The last ten seconds are counted aloud. With 'Timer voice' on,|
you hear 'Stop flying!' at zero.||
BATTERIES (bottom left):|
TX is the transmitter's battery, RX the model's. Each shows a|
bar, the total voltage and the voltage PER CELL.|
Why per cell? Most pilots know at once the cell voltage a pack|
should not go below: about 3.6 volts for LiPo and Li-ion.||
The transmitter battery always has 2 cells: LiPo, Li-ion or|
LiFePO4. Set its type, and the number of cells in the model's|
battery, on the 'Batteries' page (Transmitter setup or Model|
setup). The bars, cell voltages and warnings all use them.||
When a battery is low, Battery LOW shows and you hear a warning:|
- transmitter: at the 'Transmitter battery low at' per cent|
(Transmitter setup / 'System');|
- model: 'Stop flying!', when a cell is below|
'Stop flying volts (per cell)' (Model setup / 'Options').|
About 3.6 volts is a good start.||
LINK QUALITY (the bar beside 'Connected'):|
Green is the share of data packets that arrived, red the share|
lost, over the last 1 to 6 seconds. Set the seconds in|
'Connection quality measured over' (page 3 of 'System').||
TRIMS:|
The yellow lines down the sides and along the bottom are the|
four trims. The red dot shows where each trim is.|
They, and the 'Trims:' note, are hidden while|
'Trim multiplier (0 = off)' is 0 (Model setup / 'Options').||
LIGHT ON THE TRANSMITTER (LED):|
Blue = the radio is not trying to connect.|
Red = the radio is trying to connect.|
Green = the radio is connected.|
Blinking = binding is enabled.||
POWER BUTTON:|
With no model connected, one press switches the transmitter off.|
Held from switch-on it never switches off: that enables binding.|
With a model connected, the front screen comes back, and you|
must hold the button down while a countdown runs (TURN OFF?!).|
This guards against a knock in flight. Set the seconds in|
'Hold power button for' (page 3 of 'System').||
