Your own front screen: big, clear numbers while you fly.|
This page designs it. It shows the screen in use, live, as it|
would look in flight.|
You can design six: one for the simulator, one for a helicopter,|
one for a glider, all ready at a touch.|
Each keeps its own boxes, box sizes, box themes, dials and|
'Stays lit'. The six are kept through updates and switching off.||
THE TABS:|
The tabs 1 to 6 at the top right choose the screen. The yellow|
tab is the one in use: touch another and it is in use at once,|
and the page shows its design.|
The same tabs are on the defined front screen itself, so|
switching while you fly is one touch.||
BOXES:|
Touch a box to choose what it shows: Timer, Batteries (RX and|
TX), Link, Frame rate, Head speed, ESC temperature,|
Current, Capacity used, Bank, Rate, Motor, AMS and trims, Clock,|
Model name, Model image, Channels 1-8, Channels 1-16 or Nothing.|
Touch a choice and it is made at once.|
'OK' goes back with no change.|
A choice another box already shows is darker and carries that|
box's number, so nothing is chosen twice by mistake.||
BOX THEMES:|
In a box's chooser, press 'Theme'. A box takes any of the twelve|
themes; a theme other boxes already wear carries their numbers.|
'Same as screens' gives it the screens' theme again.|
A Motor box takes the safety's colours, as the original front|
screen's Motor box does, whatever its theme.||
ROUND DIALS:|
In a box's chooser, 'Round dial: no' / 'yes' shows that box's|
number as a needle over a round scale, with no digits (the|
Clock and the Timer get hands). Timer, Batteries, Link,|
Frame rate, Head speed, ESC temperature, Current, Capacity|
used and Clock can be dials. A scale grows to hold the biggest|
value seen. A box too small for a dial keeps its digits: give|
it a bigger box. Dials carry amber and red zones where the|
box would turn amber or red; for Head speed, Current, Capacity|
used and Frame rate, 'Red zone' in the chooser sets where the|
red starts.||
BOX SIZES:|
'Box sizes' offers twelve layouts, from one big box to nine small|
ones. The numbers grow with their boxes.|
Box 1 is at the top left, and is always one of the biggest:|
put what matters most there.||
'Stays lit: yes' stops the screen going dark while your front|
screen shows and a model is connected (or you are flying).|
Changes are kept as you make them. 'OK' closes the design page.|
'Help' opens this page; its 'Films' button plays the animation.||
WHICH FRONT SCREEN SHOWS:|
You choose on the front screens themselves:|
'Defined screens' on the original brings yours;|
'Original screen' on yours brings the original back.|
The choice stays through take-offs, landings and switching off,|
until you press the other button.|
On your screen, on the ground, tap a box twice quickly, or hold|
it for two seconds, to open its chooser there and then.||
While the safety is off, this page closes, and will not open.|
(With no safety switch: while the motor is on.)|
