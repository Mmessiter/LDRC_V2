Switches: what each switch of this transmitter makes the flight controller do - arm, rescue, self-level, the beeper, the black box and so on (the configurator's Modes tab). Needs the model connected, the safety on and a Version 2 receiver with Bluetooth.||

The arrows beside the title move between the switch actions defined. Add makes a new one: choose what it should do from the list, and it appears on the next channel, ON in the upper third of the travel. Remove takes the action showing away.||

Does: tap to choose another action from the list. On: tap for the next channel (channel 6 is AUX1).||

The bar is the switch's travel, 875 to 2125. The green zone is where the action is ON: drag the blobs to set its ends. The marker is where the switch is now, from this transmitter's own output as the flight controller sees it (988 to 2012), and the line under the bar says ON or OFF.||

The arm switch is this transmitter's safety switch: with a Rotorflight model the safety drives the arming channel (Settings). So the arm action stays on that channel, cannot be removed, and a save refuses an ON zone that reaches 988 us (the model would arm with the safety on) or stops short of 2012 us (the safety off would not arm).||

Save writes the slots that changed, stores them, and reads them back. OK leaves; edits not saved are discarded after a question.||
