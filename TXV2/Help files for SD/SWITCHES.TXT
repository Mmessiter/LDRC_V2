Any of the EIGHT switches can do any job. The top edge's four (1 to 4) are shown as they are seen from above with the sticks towards you; the front four (5 to 8) are the inputs of channels 5 to 8, and a knob in one of those places reads as a three-position switch.||

Beside each switch is where it is now: near (towards you), mid, or away. Move a switch to see which number it is.||

Touch a number or its job to choose the job: Banks 1 2 3, Bank 4 & Motor, Safety, Rates, Buddy, or Channel 5 to 16: the switch becomes that channel's input (any channel can use it on the Inputs page; on 5 to 8 it takes the place of the front switch or knob). 'Reversed' turns the switch's sense round. A switch that behaves strangely probably still needs calibrating.||

A front switch keeps feeding its channel's input whatever job it has: if that channel is in use, give it another input on the Inputs page.||

When using the Buddy box feature, one switch must be defined as 'Buddy'. It's probably best if this is a spring-return switch.||

The jobs belong to the transmitter, not to the model: they stay the same whichever model is loaded.
