The governor values Rotorflight keeps for each bank:|
the head speed, and how the governor holds it.|
The title shows the bank, such as 'Governor (bank 2)'.|
The settings shared by every bank are on 'Global ...'.|
It needs Rotorflight 2.3 (the version is in 'Settings ...').||
TO CHANGE THEM:|
1. Choose the bank with the bank switch. Wait while it loads.|
2. Touch a value, or its name, and type the new value.|
3. Press 'Save'.|
The values are written to the flight controller,|
read back to check, and stored.|
'Save' is refused while the model could be armed.||
GOOD TO KNOW:|
Save before you move the switch: unsaved changes are lost.|
'Global ...' asks first if you have unsaved changes.|
To check a save, move the switch away and back.|
The page reads the values again.|
If the page says nothing came back, do the same,|
or leave the page and open it again.|
'Save' writes only into the transmitter's bank.|
'Bank 2 / FC 1' at the top left: the flight controller is on|
bank 1. The page shows bank 1, but 'Save' writes nothing, and|
a message says so. Check the bank switch's set-up.|
(The check needs Bluetooth joined.)||
THE VALUES:|
Headspeed (rpm): the head speed this bank holds.|
Master gain: the overall strength. It scales P, I, D and F.|
P, I and D gain: how the governor corrects a head speed error.|
F gain (feed-forward): the strength of the three 'Anticipate'|
values.|
Tail-torque strength (TTA gain) and Tail-torque limit (%):|
tail torque assist (TTA). The head speed may rise to help the|
tail in hard yaw. The limit is the most it may rise.|
Max throttle (%) and Min throttle (%): the most and the least|
throttle the governor may use.|
Fall-back drop (%): how much the throttle drops if the head|
speed signal is lost (0 to 50).|
Anticipate yaw, Anticipate cyclic and Anticipate collective|
(yaw, cyclic and collective weight): how much each stick adds|
throttle before the load arrives.||
'OK' goes back to the 'Rotorflight' page.|
With unsaved changes it asks 'Save now?': 'Save' saves them,|
and the page closes once saved; 'Discard' leaves without saving.|
With no model connected the page shows the model's backup|
(see the help on the 'Rotorflight' page).|
