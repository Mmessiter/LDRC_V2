The four trims, on screen. Use them instead of the trim levers,|
or just to see the trims clearly.||
Press '+' or '-' to move a trim.|
'Reset' centres this bank's trims|
(every bank's, if 'Copy trims to all banks' is on).|
'OK' keeps the changes and goes back to 'Model setup'.||
The trims act only when the 'Trim multiplier'|
('Model setup', 'Options') is above 0.|
5 is the default, and a very good start.|
Each trim has 40 steps either side of centre.|
With 'Trim change clicks' on ('Transmitter setup',|
'Sounds etc.'), each step clicks,|
and a beep marks the centre and each end.||
For a helicopter with a flybarless system, turn the trims off:|
set the 'Trim multiplier' to 0, and set all subtrims to 0.||
BANKS:|
With 'Copy trims to all banks' off ('Model setup', 'Options'),|
the trims apply ONLY to the bank in use, shown top left.||
MOVE TO SUBTRIM:|
Once the trims are right, 'Move to subtrim' moves|
this bank's four trims into the subtrims. It asks first.|
The four trims are then centred in every bank.|
Subtrims act in every bank alike, so this bank flies as before.|
Other banks lose any trims of their own.|
Other channels' subtrims do not change.|
A subtrim already set is added to.|
Subtrims always use a multiplier of 5. The move allows for|
any other 'Trim multiplier', so the servos stay where they were.||
TAKE CARE:|
Each trim goes only into the subtrim of its 'Trim out' channel|
('Model setup', 'Inputs').|
A second servo on the same stick (two aileron servos, say)|
loses that trim: set its subtrim by hand.|
In Mode 2, do not use it for now: the elevator and throttle|
trims would go into each other's subtrim.|
Check each control before you fly.||
TRIM LEVERS:|
If a trim lever moves its trim the wrong way, set the|
directions again: 'Transmitter setup', 'Calibrate',|
'Calibrate trims ...'.|
