Every setting of the model in use starts here.|
The transmitter's own settings are on 'Transmitter setup'.|
'OK' goes back to the Front screen.||
WHERE TO FIND THINGS:|
'Options': trims, motor switch, timer, warnings and picture.|
'Channels': watch each channel move, and edit its curves.|
'Inputs': what moves each channel, its name and its output.|
'Servo speed': slow a servo down (flaps, undercarriage).|
'Bank names': a name for each of the four banks.|
'Model IDs': store the ID of this model's receiver.|
'Rotorflight': the flight controller's settings.||
RECEIVER UPDATES:|
'Receiver updates' checks messiter.com for receiver firmware.|
Nothing is installed until you press 'Install now'.||
It needs the model switched on and connected.|
(Until then the button is grey.)|
The safety must be on.|
(With no safety switch, the motor must be off.)|
It works with a Version 2 receiver only.|
The transmitter needs its own WiFi for the check:|
'Transmitter setup', 'WiFi'.||
On 'Install now' it sends the order by radio,|
then goes silent for about a minute.|
Meanwhile the receiver fetches the update over the WiFi|
it knows, installs it and restarts.|
Keep the model switched on, and the safety on.||
At the field, give the receiver your phone's hotspot once,|
on the phone app's 'WiFi settings' page.|
