Give each of this model's four banks (flight modes) a name for what|
it does. The name shows on the front screen and other pages, and|
is spoken when you change bank.||
TO NAME THE BANKS:|
- Scroll each bank's list to the name you want.|
- Or press a set of four: 'Default' (Flight mode 1 to 4), 'Vbar'|
(Bank 1 to 4), 'Heli' (Hover, Idle up 1, Idle up 2, Hold) or|
'Glider' (Launch, Normal, Speed, Thermal).|
- 'Listen' plays the four names in turn.|
- Press 'OK' to save.||
The names come from a fixed list: each has its own spoken sound.|
To silence them, turn off 'Bank voice' ('Transmitter setup',|
then 'Sounds etc.'). That also silences the safety, motor|
and rates voices.|
