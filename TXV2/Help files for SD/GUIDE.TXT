Every setup step in the order Rotorflight's own setup|
takes them, for a new helicopter (or a check of an old one).||
Touch a step to open its page. OK on that page brings you|
back here, and the step is ticked.|
'Go to step ...' opens the first step not ticked yet; the|
words above it say what that step is for.||
The ticks are kept for each model, so you can stop and come|
back later. 'Start again' clears them (nothing in the model|
changes).||
Fly gently at first. After any change: 'Check my setup', and|
back up again.|
