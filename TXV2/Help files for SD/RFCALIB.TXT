Teaches the flight controller which way is level, for|
self-levelling and rescue. The same in every bank|
('All banks' at the top left).||
It needs the safety on (model disarmed) and a Version 2|
receiver with Bluetooth. 'Calibrate level' and the live level|
need the model connected.|
With no model, the level trims can still be changed in the|
model's backup on the card, if one was made. The change is|
then offered to the model when it next connects.||
TO CALIBRATE THE LEVEL:|
1. Stand the board (or the helicopter) flat and perfectly still.|
2. Tap 'Calibrate level' and answer 'OK'.|
3. Keep it still for a few seconds, until it says Calibrated.|
The new level is then stored in the flight controller.|
Best done on a flat desk, before the board goes into the model:|
a helicopter rarely sits truly level. With the board flat, its|
plugs may face the front or the back: it makes no difference.||
THE LEVEL LINES:|
They show the roll and pitch the flight controller sees, live.|
The second line says Level when both are within 2 degrees.|
Just after a calibration, both read near 0.||
LEVEL TRIMS:|
Pitch trim and Roll trim (deg): small corrections to the level|
that self-levelling and rescue aim for (-30 to 30, one decimal).|
Tap a number to type a new one.|
A switch or knob can change them in flight too, with|
'Adjustments ...' on the Rotorflight page.|
'Save' appears once a trim is changed. It writes the trims to|
the flight controller, stores them there and reads them back.||
'OK' leaves. With a change not saved it asks 'Save now?':|
'Save' saves, then leaves; 'Discard' leaves without saving.|
It waits while a calibration or a save is running.|
