TO ADD YOUR OWN PHOTO:|
1. Press 'Add photo'.|
2. Scan the code with your phone's camera, or type the address|
shown into the phone's web browser.|
3. On the phone, choose a photo and fit it in the frame.|
4. On the phone, press 'Send to the transmitter'.|
5. On the transmitter, press 'OK' to use it.||
The phone must be on the same WiFi as the transmitter.|
If the transmitter is not on WiFi, press 'WiFi setup'.|
While the model could fly, the WiFi stays off. The page then says|
what to do first: motor off, safety on, wait, or model off.|
The code works only while this page is open.||
The phone makes the photo the right size and names it after the|
model. You can change the name: 8 letters or digits at most.|
A photo sent again with the same name replaces your old one.|
A photo with the name of one of the other pictures is shown|
in its place.||
Your own photos have green names. 'Delete' removes one of your|
own. It asks first: press 'Delete' again, or 'No' to keep it.|
The other pictures came with the transmitter.||
All the pictures are kept on the screen's own card,|
not on the main board's card.|
