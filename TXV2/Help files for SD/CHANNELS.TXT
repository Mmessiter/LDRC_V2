Where all 16 channels are now, live. From here you can edit|
the curve of any channel, in any bank.||
TO EDIT A CURVE:|
1. Check the right model is loaded (its name: yellow, top left).|
2. Choose the bank with the bank switch (its name is top left).|
3. Press the channel's button: odd numbers on the left, even|
on the right. The arrow on each button points to its bar.||
Each bar grows from the middle line:|
to the left below centre, to the right above.||
THE NUMBERS:|
Beside each bar, by its button: what that channel sends now,|
in microseconds. 1500 is the middle.|
Rotorflight on CRSF (the receiver's usual way) sees 1000 to|
2000 as 989 to 2012, and anything outside as the nearer end.||
MAX RANGE:|
At the bottom: the range this model's channels can use, and|
what the curve pages show bottom to top. Touch one to choose:|
it asks first.|
500 to 2500: the widest, as ever.|
945 to 2055: just past the 1000 to 2000 Rotorflight reads. A|
curve taken to the top and bottom then gives its full range even|
with a stick that stops a little short.|
A change keeps every point's value; one beyond the new range|
moves to its edge. Check every channel after a change.||
There are 64 curves: 16 channels x 4 banks.|
To give every bank the same curve, turn on 'Copy to all banks'|
on the curve page before you press 'OK'.|
This is often what you want.||
RATES:|
'Rates' (Model setup) sets three rates for up to eight channels.|
Each rate is 0 to 200 per cent.|
They work in every bank: 3 rates and 4 banks give 12 set-ups.|
