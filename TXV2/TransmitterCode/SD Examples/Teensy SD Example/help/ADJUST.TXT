Adjustments: a knob or a switch on this transmitter changes a Rotorflight setting in flight (the configurator's Adjustments tab). Needs the model connected, the safety on, and the USB-C cable from the receiver to the flight controller: Rotorflight cannot send its adjustments over the receiver's lead.||

The arrows beside the title move between the adjustments defined. Add makes a new one after the one showing; Remove takes it away.||

Changes: tap the setting to choose another from the list (rates and PIDs first). Tap the bank box for "in bank 1" and so on, or "in any bank": a bank needs a 'PID bank switch (1 to 6)' adjustment first, which is how Rotorflight knows what the switch means. A bank switch has no bank box: it is the bank.||

The line under the setting: tap the first box for a Knob (the whole travel maps the low end value to the high end), a Switch with 2, 3 or 4 positions, or Step up / down (a three-position switch: up adds the step, down takes it, within the lowest and highest values). Tap the channel box for the next channel (channel 6 is AUX1).||

A switch: give the first and last positions their values; Rotorflight spaces the positions evenly between them, and the zones show the value each gets. The round blobs are the ends of the switch's travel: drag them to where its first and last positions are (a real switch spans about 988 to 2012). Rotorflight keeps a switch as one line, the travel mapped onto the values, so it works at every change. One line per position, which the transmitter wrote until 9 October 2026, worked once per power-up only: such lines are converted when the page opens - press Save.||

The bank switch: its zones are matched to this transmitter's own banks. Each bank of a model sends its own value on the bank channel (bank 1 the lowest), and the transmitter sets the ends to bank 1's value and the last bank's, so every bank falls in its own zone. Flip through the banks and the line under the bar says which bank Rotorflight picks. If a bank cannot be told apart, the page says so: space the banks' values evenly on that channel in Model setup.||

The bar is the switch or knob's travel, 875 to 2125. Tap a zone to type its value. The green marker is where the channel is now, from this transmitter's own output as the flight controller sees it, and the line under the bar says what that gives.||

Save writes all 42 of Rotorflight's lines, stores them, and reads them back. A rate (top rotation speed) is shown in degrees per second as on the Rates page.||

OK leaves; edits not saved are discarded after a question.||
