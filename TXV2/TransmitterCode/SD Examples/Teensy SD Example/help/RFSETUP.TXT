Rotorflight settings: what the transmitter itself needs to know about this model's flight controller.||

Link rates and banks: On makes the rates bank follow the PID bank (the bank switch sets both). Off leaves the rates bank to the rates switch.||

Version: the Rotorflight version the receiver reports from the flight controller (2.2 or 2.3). 0.0 means it has not answered yet; the model keeps the last version it knew.||

Arming channel: the channel the safety switch drives to arm the flight controller (usually 6). Press the box to type another.||

Main RPM ratio: the main rotor's gear ratio, motor turns per rotor turn, so the head speed can be shown. Press the box to type it.||

OK saves these with the model and returns to the Rotorflight options.||
