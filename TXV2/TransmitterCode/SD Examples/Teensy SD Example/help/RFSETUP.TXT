What the transmitter itself needs to know|
about this model's flight controller.|
Touch a white box to type a new value.|
'OK' saves them with the model,|
and goes back to the Rotorflight options.||
LINK RATES AND BANKS:|
On: the bank switch sets the rates too (Bank 2 uses Rate 2).|
Off: the 'Rates' switch sets the rates on its own.|
The switch is saved as soon as you touch it.||
ROTORFLIGHT VERSION:|
The version the receiver reports from the flight controller:|
2.2 or 2.3. It cannot be typed.|
0.0 means no version is known yet.|
Once one is known, the model keeps it,|
even while the flight controller is silent.|
At 0.0 the safety switch does not drive the arming channel,|
and 'Link rates and banks' has no effect.|
At 2.2 or 2.3 the transmitter's own rate percentages|
are not used: Rotorflight's rates are.|
'Governor ...' needs 2.3.|
The columns of the Rates page follow the version too.||
ARMING CHANNEL:|
The channel the safety switch drives (usually 6).|
Safety off arms the flight controller.|
1 to 16. Any other entry is ignored: the old channel stays.||
MAIN GEAR RATIO:|
Motor turns for one turn of the main rotor, such as 10.3.|
A Version 1 receiver uses it to work out the head speed.|
A Version 2 receiver does not: Rotorflight sends the head speed.|
With a Version 2 receiver, set Rotorflight's own gear ratio on|
'Model basics ...' (its second page), not here.||
MODEL BASICS:|
'Model basics ...' opens two pages for a new model's set-up:|
swash type, which way the rotor turns, tail type, ESC signal|
and brand, motor pole count, and the main and tail gear ratios.|
They need the model (or its backup on the card).|
A 'Save' there restarts the flight controller.|
