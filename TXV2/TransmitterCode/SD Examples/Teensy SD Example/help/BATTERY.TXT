Tell the flight controller about the flight pack, for its|
warnings and the telemetry. The settings are the same in every|
bank ('All banks' at the top left).||
It needs the model connected, with the safety on,|
and a Version 2 receiver with Bluetooth.|
With no model, the page shows the backup on the card.|
'Save' then changes the backup.|
The change is offered to the model when it next connects.||
Tap any number to type a new one. Tap the box beside|
'Voltage from' or 'Current from' to step through its choices.||
FLIGHT PACK:|
Cell count (0 = auto): how many cells the pack has, up to 24.|
0 = automatic: the flight controller counts them by the voltage.|
Capacity (mAh): the size of the pack, for the fuel gauge.|
With several battery profiles, it is the one in use.|
Voltage from and Current from: usually ESC telemetry;|
FC pads (ADC) when the battery wires go to the flight|
controller's own sensor pads; or Nowhere.|
(FBUS shows only if it was already chosen.)||
CELL VOLTAGES (V):|
Volts per cell, 1.00 to 5.00.|
Full: fresh off the charger (4.20 for LiPo).|
Warning: time to land (3.50 is common).|
Empty: the bottom of the fuel gauge.|
Highest allowed: used for counting the cells.|
They must rise: Empty below Warning below Full, and Full no|
higher than Highest allowed. 'Save' refuses otherwise.||
SENSORS:|
'Sensors >' shows when a source is FC pads (ADC).|
Its page, 'Battery sensors (FC pads)', has the voltage|
sensor's scale, and the current sensor's scale and offset.|
Each sensor shows only when its source is FC pads (ADC).|
Adjust them until the flight controller agrees with a meter.|
'< Battery' goes back. 'Save' and 'OK' work from either page.||
SAVE AND OK:|
'Save' appears once something is changed. It writes to the|
flight controller, stores and reads back.|
'OK' leaves. With a change not saved it asks 'Save now?':|
'Save' saves, then leaves; 'Discard' leaves without saving.|
