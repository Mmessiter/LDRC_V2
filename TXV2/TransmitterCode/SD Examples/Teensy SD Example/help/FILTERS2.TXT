What the configurator's expert mode adds to its Gyro tab.|
Rarely needed: the defaults suit most helicopters.||
Tap a number to type a new one. Tap Filter type to switch|
between 1st and 2nd order.|
Each section's Enable turns it on or off (green = on). Off sets|
its values to 0 and hides its rows. On again before you leave|
these pages, it gets its values back; otherwise it starts from|
the configurator's defaults.|
'Save' appears once something is changed. It writes both filter|
pages, stores them and reads them back. If the first page was|
changed too, it asks first.|
'< Previous' goes back to the first page, changes kept.|
'OK' leaves; if a change was not saved, it asks first.||
LOWPASS 1 DYNAMIC CUTOFF:|
The first page's lowpass cutoff follows the head speed, from|
Min cutoff to Max cutoff (0 to 1000 Hz), instead of staying|
fixed. Enable off = the fixed cutoff.|
Min cutoff must be below Max cutoff, or it counts as off.|
Turning the first page's lowpass off turns this off too.||
LOWPASS FILTER 2:|
A second lowpass after the first, 1st or 2nd order, with its|
own cutoff (0 to 1000 Hz). Off on most helicopters.||
NOTCH FILTER 1 AND 2:|
A notch at one fixed frequency, for a vibration that stays put|
whatever the head speed. Enable off = no notch.|
Centre frequency is the middle of the notch, and Cutoff|
frequency is where it starts (0 to 1000 Hz each).|
For example, centre 260 and cutoff 160 cover 160 to 360 Hz.|
