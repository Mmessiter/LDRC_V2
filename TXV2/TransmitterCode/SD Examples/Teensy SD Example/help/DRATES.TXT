Rates make chosen channels move less (or more) for the|
same stick movement.|
There are three rates, each 0 to 200%. One is normally 100%.||
TO SET UP RATES:|
1. Touch the box beside 'Rate 1 (%)', 'Rate 2 (%)' and 'Rate 3 (%)'|
in turn, and type each percentage.|
2. Under 'Channels using these rates', touch a box and type|
a channel number: up to eight, usually aileron, elevator and|
rudder. 0 = not used.|
Each channel's name appears beside its number.|
3. Press 'Save' to keep your changes and stay, or 'OK' to|
keep them and leave.||
WHICH RATE IS USED:|
The switch with the 'Rates' job picks the rate: away is rate 1,|
mid is rate 2, near is rate 3.|
The 'Rates' job is set in 'Transmitter setup', then 'Switches'.|
With no 'Rates' switch, every channel gets 100%.|
Channels not in the list always get 100%.||
THE LIST OF CHOICES:|
The list ('Use switch', 'Use rate 1', 'Use rate 2', 'Use rate 3')|
is meant to let each bank choose: follow the rates switch,|
or always use one rate.|
It sets the bank in use now, named in the line under the|
channels. Move the bank switch to set another bank.|
For now the transmitter keeps this choice but does not act on it:|
in flight, the rates switch alone picks the rate.||
HOW A RATE WORKS:|
A rate shrinks the channel's curve towards the middle (or, over|
100%, stretches it).|
At 50% the servo moves half as far.|
The same three rates apply in every bank.||
ROTORFLIGHT MODELS:|
A Rotorflight model does not use these percentages.|
Set its rates on 'Rotorflight', then 'Rates ...'.|
There, the 'Rates' switch picks which rate you see and edit.|
With 'Link rates and banks' on ('Settings ...'), the bank switch|
picks it instead.|
