How the sticks reach the swash and the tail, with the phone|
app's names (its 'Travel extents' page).|
The same in every bank ('All banks' at the top left).||
It needs the safety on (model disarmed) and a Version 2|
receiver with Bluetooth. With no model, the pages show the|
model's backup on the card, if one was made: a 'Save' then|
changes the backup, and the change is offered to the model|
when it next connects.||
Four pages: this one, 'Travel extents: swash',|
'Travel extents: tail' and 'Bench measure' (the swash held at|
an angle for a pitch gauge).|
'Next >' and '< Previous' move between them, changes kept.|
Tap a number to type a new one.|
Tap a switch to change it (green = on).|
'Save' appears once something is changed. It writes all the|
pages to the flight controller, stores them there and reads|
them back. It asks first, because it changes how far the swash|
and tail move: check them on the bench, blades off.|
'OK' leaves. With a change not saved it asks 'Save now?':|
'Save' saves, then leaves; 'Discard' leaves without saving.||
MAIN ROTOR SETTINGS:|
Swashplate type and Main rotor direction show what the flight|
controller has. To change them: 'Settings ...', then|
'First-time basics ...' (the flight controller restarts).|
Aileron, Elevator and Collective reversed (green = reversed):|
use one when that control moves the wrong way. It reverses|
that control only: the servos and the other controls stay|
as they are.|
Before flying, check on the bench that the swash moves the|
right way for each stick.||
SWASH TRIMS (%):|
Roll, Pitch and Collective trim: small corrections that level|
the swash (-100 to 100, one decimal; 1 = 1% of servo throw).||
LEVEL THE SWASH BY A TEST LIFT-OFF:|
A swash levelled on the bench is seldom quite level, and the|
air shows it. In still air, lift off gently with the aileron|
and elevator sticks untouched. A level swash climbs straight|
up. A new model heads off forwards, backwards or sideways|
until you correct it.|
Land. Move the trim of that axis a little (Pitch for forwards|
or backwards, Roll for sideways; 2 or 3 at a time), save, and|
lift off again. Heading off faster? Go the other way. Repeat|
until it climbs straight.|
A faint drift to one side is normal: the tail rotor's own|
thrust pushes the whole model that way. Leave it be.|
Trim here, not with the transmitter's trims or the servo|
centres. A trim here is a fixed tilt of the swash, which the|
flight controller never sees: on the ground its corrections|
drain away, so at lift-off the swash sits where these trims|
put it. A mis-trim tips the model before the flight controller|
catches it, and it then holds that lean, not the level.|
