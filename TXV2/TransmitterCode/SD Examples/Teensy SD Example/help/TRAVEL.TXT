Mixer: how the sticks reach the swash and the tail, laid out as the configurator's Mixer tab, with its names. Needs a Version 2 receiver with Bluetooth, the safety on and the model disarmed. Not per bank. Three pages: Next and Previous.||

Main Rotor Settings. Swashplate Type and Main Rotor Direction are shown as the flight controller has them; they are set in the configurator. Aileron, Elevator and Collective reversed: the configurator's Control Direction (Normal or Reverse), a switch each: green = reversed. Check the swash moves the right way on the bench before flying.||

Swashplate Trims: Roll trim, Pitch trim and Collective trim, in percent, to one decimal, to level the swash.||

Press a number to type a new one. Save writes everything on all three pages to the flight controller, asks first, stores there, and reads back. OK leaves; if something was edited and not saved, it asks.||
