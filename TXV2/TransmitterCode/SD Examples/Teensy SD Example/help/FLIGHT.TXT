This is your own front screen: big, clear numbers while you fly.||

'Transmitter' and 'Model' open the setups, as on the original front screen.||

'Original screen' shows the original front screen. Its 'Defined screen' button brings this screen back. Your choice stays, through take-offs, landings and switching off, until you press the other button.||

A box turns amber or red when its value needs a look. The top line turns red for BATTERY LOW, or NO LINK in flight.||

To choose what the boxes show, their themes and their sizes: Transmitter setup, Appearance, then Defined front screen. There are six screens to design, and the tabs 1 to 6 at the top of this screen switch between them at a touch; the yellow tab is the one in use, and each keeps its own design. (The time is a box you can add; the tabs took its place in the top line.)
