Your own front screen (a defined front screen): big, clear|
numbers while you fly.|
The top line shows the model's name and the bank.||
BUTTONS:|
'Transmitter' and 'Model' open the setups.|
'Original screen' shows the original front screen.|
Its 'Defined screens' button brings this one back.|
Your choice stays through take-offs, landings and switching off,|
until you press the other button.|
The tabs 1 to 6 (top right) switch between your six screens at|
a touch, even in flight. The yellow tab is the one in use.||
TO CHANGE A BOX FROM HERE:|
On the ground, press and hold the box for two full seconds.|
Its chooser opens. Your choice brings this screen straight back.|
A tap or a slide does nothing.|
(On the ground means the safety is on. With no safety switch,|
it means the motor is off.)||
WARNING COLOURS:|
A box turns amber or red, with a white frame, when its value|
needs a look:|
- Battery: amber at 40 per cent or less, red at 20 or less.|
- Link: amber below 75 per cent, red below 50.|
- ESC temperature: amber from 80 degrees, red from 95.|
The top line turns red for BATTERY LOW, or for NO LINK in|
flight (the Link box then says LOST).||
WHAT THE BOXES SHOW:|
A battery box: the voltage per cell, big; under it, the total|
voltage and the per cent.|
A box shows -- when there is nothing to show. Its small line|
says 'no model' on the ground, or 'no link' in flight.|
Head speed, current, capacity used and|
ESC temperature need Rotorflight.|
A Motor box takes the safety's colours, as the original front|
screen's Motor box does.||
While you hold the power button to switch off, the original|
front screen shows, with its countdown.||
TO DESIGN THE SCREENS:|
Transmitter setup / 'Appearance' / 'Defined front screens'.|
There you choose what each box shows, the box sizes and the box|
themes, for each of the six screens.|
For the time of day, give a box the 'Clock'.|
