'Original screen' shows the original front screen. Its 'Defined screen' button brings this screen back. Your choice stays, through take-offs, landings and switching off, until you press the other button.||

To choose what the boxes show, their themes and their sizes: Transmitter setup, Appearance, then Defined front screen. There are six screens to design: the tabs 1 to 6 on that page choose which one you are looking at now, and each keeps its own design.
