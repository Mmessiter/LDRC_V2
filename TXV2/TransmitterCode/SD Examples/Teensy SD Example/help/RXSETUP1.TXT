This model's trims, motor switch, timer and warnings.|
Touch a box to type a new value.|
Touch a switch to turn it on or off.|
'OK' keeps the changes and goes to the Front screen.||
TRIMS:|
Trim multiplier (0 = off): how far each trim step moves.|
5 is a very good default. Use 0 on a flybarless helicopter.|
Copy trims to all banks: on, a trim change goes to every bank.|
Off, each bank keeps its own trims.||
MOTOR:|
Throttle channel: the channel the motor (ESC) is on. Usually 3.|
Motor off value: what the throttle channel is sent, in|
microseconds, while the motor is off or the safety is on.|
833 is the default, and nearly always right (it was -67|
in the old units). 500 is the lowest, 2500 the highest.|
It is not reversed with the channel:|
if you reverse the throttle, change this value to match.||
Use motor kill switch: on, the transmitter checks the motor|
switch before it connects: at switch-on, or after a lost link.|
While the motor is on, it says 'The motor switch is ON.'|
and waits until you switch it off.|
The motor switch has the job 'Bank 4 & Motor'|
('Transmitter setup', 'Switches'). Its three positions:|
Away = bank 1, 2 or 3, motor on.|
Mid = bank 4, motor on.|
Near = bank 4, motor OFF.||
TIMER:|
The timer runs while the motor is on, and pauses when it is off.|
Timer counts down: on, the timer counts down from|
'Count down from (minutes)' and says the last ten seconds.|
With 'Timer voice' on ('Transmitter setup', 'Sounds etc.'),|
it then says 'Stop flying!'.|
Off, it counts up, and the minutes box is grey.||
WARNINGS:|
Stop flying volts (per cell): below this, per cell,|
the transmitter says 'Stop flying!'.|
It watches the model's battery, as the receiver measures it.|
When the motor stops, the voltage recovers a little.|
So set this a little lower than you might think.|
Find the best value by trying: every model differs.||
Volts correction (x 0.01 V): corrects a small error|
in that battery reading, in hundredths of a volt per cell.|
For example, 5 adds 0.05 V to each cell. It may be negative.|
Compare with a good voltmeter, and adjust until the two agree.||
Current warning (A, 0 = off): above this current, the|
transmitter sounds a warning and shows 'Current too high!'.|
It warns once each time the model connects. 0 turns it off.||
MORE:|
'Model picture ...', or a touch on the picture,|
chooses this model's picture.|
'Servo types ...': each servo's pulse rate and centre,|
for a Version 1 receiver.||
The receiver's output (SBUS, CRSF, PPM ...) is not set here.|
A Version 1 receiver always sends SBUS (all 16 channels)|
as well as its servo outputs.|
On a Version 2 receiver, choose it on the phone app's|
'Output protocol' page.|
