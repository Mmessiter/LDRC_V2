How the screens look: the background picture, the colours|
(themes) and your own front screens.|
Each of the three has a page of its own; 'Themes' and 'Defined|
front screens' have help and animations of their own too.||
BACKGROUND PICTURE:|
The picture behind the pages. '-' and '+' step through the|
43 pictures; 'OK' keeps the one showing.||
THEMES:|
Twelve themes, each a panel colour with its own text colour.|
Touch one: the page shows it at once, framed in yellow.|
'OK' makes it the screens' theme.|
'Edit themes' changes either colour of any theme to any colour.|
A box on your own front screen can wear a theme of its own.||
DEFINED FRONT SCREENS:|
Your own front screen: big, clear numbers while you fly.|
'Defined front screens' opens the design page: it shows the|
screen in use, live. You can design six, for different models,|
ready at a touch of the tabs 1 to 6.|
Touch a box to choose what it shows; 'Box sizes' offers twelve|
layouts; any number can be a round dial with red zones.||
WHICH FRONT SCREEN SHOWS:|
You choose on the front screens themselves:|
'Defined screens' on the original brings yours;|
'Original screen' on yours brings the original back.|
The choice stays through take-offs, landings and switching off,|
until you press the other button.||
'Help' opens this page; its 'Films' button plays the animation.|
'OK' goes back to Transmitter setup.||
While the safety is off, the 'Appearance', 'Themes' and design|
pages close, and will not open.|
(With no safety switch: while the motor is on.)|
