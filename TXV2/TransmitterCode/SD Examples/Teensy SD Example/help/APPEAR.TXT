How the screens look.||

BACKGROUND PICTURE: the picture behind the screens. '+' and '-' choose it, 'OK' keeps it.||

THEMES: twelve themes, each a panel colour with its own text colour. Touch one: the page shows it at once. 'OK' makes it the screens' theme, 'Cancel' leaves things as they were.||

'Edit themes' changes either colour of any theme to any colour at all: touch a theme, press 'Panel colour' or 'Text colour', then slide the three bars - colour, strength (grey to vivid), light (black to bright). The sample shows the theme as it will look. 'Defaults' puts all twelve back as they came.||

The themes go by number, 1 to 12. Buttons, the white boxes you type into, and red and green words keep their own colours.||

DEFINED FRONT SCREEN: your own front screen, with big, clear numbers while you fly.||

SIX SCREENS: you can design six of them, numbered 1 to 6 on the tabs at the top right of the setup page. The yellow tab is the screen in use; touch another tab and that screen is in use at once, and the page shows its design. Each of the six keeps its own choice of boxes, box sizes, box themes and 'Stays lit', so a design for the simulator, one for a helicopter and one for a glider can all be ready, and switching between them is one touch instead of designing again. The six are kept in the screen through updates and switching off.||

Touch a box to choose what it shows: the timer, a battery, the link, the frame rate, head speed, ESC temperature, current, capacity used, bank, rate, motor, the clock, the model's name, the model's picture, or the channel bars (channels 1 to 8, or all 16).||

'Box sizes' offers twelve layouts, from one big box to nine small ones. Box 1 is always the biggest: put what matters most there. The numbers grow with their boxes.||

A choice another box already shows is darker and carries that box's number, so nothing is chosen twice by mistake. A Motor box takes the safety's colours, as the original front screen's motor button does, whatever theme it was given. Box themes: touch a box, then 'Theme'. A box takes any of the twelve themes; a theme other boxes already wear carries their numbers. 'Same as screens' gives it the screens' theme again.||

Which front screen shows is chosen on the front screens themselves: 'Defined screen' on the original brings yours, 'Original screen' on yours brings the original back, and the choice stays, through take-offs, landings and switching off, until you press the other button. Your screen's other buttons: 'Transmitter', 'Model' and 'Help'. A touch anywhere else does nothing.||

'Stays lit: yes' keeps the screen from going dark while it shows. A warning (BATTERY LOW, NO LINK) turns its top line red, and the power-off countdown always shows on the original front screen.
