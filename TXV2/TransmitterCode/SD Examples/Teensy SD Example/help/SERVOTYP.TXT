For servos that need a pulse rate (Hz) or a centre pulse|
(us, microseconds) other than the usual 50 Hz and 1500 us.|
Check the servo's instructions for the right values.||
Touch a box to type a new value.|
'Defaults' sets 50 Hz and 1500 us on every channel.|
'OK' saves the values, sends them to the receiver|
and goes to the Front screen.|
They are sent again each time the model connects.||
These are for the servo outputs of a Version 1 receiver.|
A Version 2 receiver ignores them.||
SHARED TIMERS:|
Pairs marked * or ** share one timer in the receiver,|
so each pair runs at one rate.|
Channels 3 and 4 are marked *, channels 7 and 10 **.|
To give the rudder a different rate from the throttle,|
move the rudder to another output on the 'Inputs' page.||
A centre below 1000 (such as 760) is for narrow-pulse servos.|
