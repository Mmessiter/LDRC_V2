Calibrate: teaches the flight controller which way is level, for self-levelling and rescue. Needs the model connected, the safety on and a Version 2 receiver with Bluetooth.||

The level line shows the roll and pitch the flight controller sees, live. Calibrate level: stand the board (or the helicopter) flat and perfectly still and confirm; it takes two seconds and is stored. Best done on a flat desk before the board goes into the model - a helicopter rarely sits truly level. Front or back mounting does not matter.||

Level trims: small corrections, in degrees, to the level that self-levelling and rescue aim for (-30 to 30). The adjustments can change them in flight too. Save writes, stores and reads them back; with no model they are changed in the backup, to be written at the next connection.||

OK leaves.||
