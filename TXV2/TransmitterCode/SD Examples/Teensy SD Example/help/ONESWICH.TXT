Choose the job of this switch. One job per switch; giving a job to this switch takes it from any other that had it.||

Banks 1 2 3: up, mid and down choose the bank. Bank 4 & Motor: down is the motor on; mid is bank 4 with the motor on; up is bank 4 with the motor off. Safety: the up position is 'safety off'. Rates: three rates. Buddy: hand control to the buddy. Channel 5 to 16: the switch becomes that channel's input, which any channel may use on the Inputs page; a switch given channel 5 to 8 takes the place of the front switch or knob there.||

'Reversed' turns the switch's sense round, so that up and down swap.||

Switches 5 to 8 are the front four, the inputs of channels 5 to 8 (a knob there reads as a switch). A job on one of them does not stop it feeding its channel.
