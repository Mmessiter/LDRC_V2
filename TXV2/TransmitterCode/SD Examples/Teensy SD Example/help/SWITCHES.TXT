What the switches do: 'Switches' on 'Transmitter setup' gives|
each of the eight switches its job.|
(What a switch makes the flight controller do - arm, rescue, the|
bank - is 'Switches & knobs' on the Rotorflight menu: its own help.)||
THE TRANSMITTER'S SWITCHES:|
Any of the eight switches can do any job. Touch a switch's number|
or its job to choose the job.|
Switches 1 to 4 are on the top edge, shown as seen from above with|
the sticks towards you.|
Switches 5 to 8 are on the front: the inputs of channels 5 to 8.|
Given a job, a knob there works as a three-position switch.|
Beside each job is where that switch is now: near (towards you),|
mid or away. Move a switch to see which number it is.|
'R' after a job means that switch is reversed.|
If a front switch shows no position, calibrate:|
'Transmitter setup', then 'Calibrate'.||
A front switch with no other job is its channel's input,|
marked 'Channel 5' to 'Channel 8' (or the channel's name,|
if you renamed it).|
Any other job takes it from its channel, whose input then sits|
at its centre. Give that channel another input on the 'Inputs'|
page if you need one.|
'Not used' does the same and nothing else.||
For the buddy box, one switch needs the 'Buddy' job.|
A spring-return switch is best: letting go gives control back to|
the instructor at once.|
The jobs belong to the transmitter, not the model: they stay the|
same whichever model is loaded.|
'OK' keeps them and goes back to 'Transmitter setup'.||
