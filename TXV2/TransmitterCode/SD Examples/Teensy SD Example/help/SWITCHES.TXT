What the switches do. Two pages open this help:|
'Switches' on 'Transmitter setup' gives each of the eight switches|
its job: read THE TRANSMITTER'S SWITCHES, just below.|
'Switches ...' on the Rotorflight menu sets what a switch makes|
the flight controller do: read ROTORFLIGHT SWITCHES, further on.||
THE TRANSMITTER'S SWITCHES:|
Any of the eight switches can do any job. Touch a switch's number|
or its job to choose the job.|
Switches 1 to 4 are on the top edge, shown as seen from above with|
the sticks towards you.|
Switches 5 to 8 are on the front: the inputs of channels 5 to 8.|
Given a job, a knob there works as a three-position switch.|
Beside each job is where that switch is now: near (towards you),|
mid or away. Move a switch to see which number it is.|
'R' after a job means that switch is reversed.|
If a front switch shows no position, calibrate:|
'Transmitter setup', then 'Calibrate'.||
A front switch with no other job is its channel's input,|
marked 'Channel 5' to 'Channel 8' (or the channel's name,|
if you renamed it).|
Any other job takes it from its channel, whose input then sits|
at its centre. Give that channel another input on the 'Inputs'|
page if you need one.|
'Not used' does the same and nothing else.||
For the buddy box, one switch needs the 'Buddy' job.|
A spring-return switch is best: letting go gives control back to|
the instructor at once.|
The jobs belong to the transmitter, not the model: they stay the|
same whichever model is loaded.|
'OK' keeps them and goes back to 'Transmitter setup'.||
ROTORFLIGHT SWITCHES:|
On the Rotorflight menu, 'Switches ...' sets what each switch makes|
the flight controller do: arm, rescue, self-level, the beeper,|
the black box and more (the Configurator's Modes tab).|
It needs the safety on. With the model connected, it also needs|
a Version 2 receiver with Bluetooth.|
With no model, it uses the model's backup on the card, if there|
is one. A 'Save' then changes the backup, and the change is|
offered to the model when it next connects.||
THE PAGE:|
'<' and '>' beside the title step through the switch actions.|
Tap the box beside 'Does' to choose another action. Drag the|
list until it is in the middle, then press 'OK'. 'Cancel'|
keeps the old one.|
Tap the box beside 'on' for the next channel, 6 to 16|
(after 16 it goes back to 6). Channel 6 is AUX1 in Rotorflight.|
'Add' makes a new action: choose it from the list. It|
goes on the channel after the one showing, ON in the upper|
third of the travel.|
Rotorflight holds up to 20 switch actions.|
'Remove' takes away the action showing, after a question.||
THE BAR:|
The bar is the channel's travel, 875 to 2125 microseconds (us).|
The green zone is where the action is ON.|
Drag the round ends to set the zone.|
The marker line (white, edged in black) is where the switch is|
now: this transmitter's output, as the flight controller sees|
it (988 to 2012 us).|
The line under the bar says ON or OFF.||
THE ARM SWITCH:|
The arm switch is this transmitter's safety switch. With a|
Rotorflight model, the safety drives the arming channel (set|
in 'Settings ...').|
So the arm action is kept on that channel: if the flight|
controller had it elsewhere, the page moves it there and says|
so; 'Save' writes it. Change the channel in 'Settings ...'.|
It cannot be swapped for another action, or removed.|
'Save' refuses an ON zone that reaches 988 us: the model would|
arm with the safety on.|
It also refuses one that stops short of 2012 us (safety off would|
not arm), and a set with no arm action.||
SAVE AND OK:|
'Save' appears once something is changed. It writes the actions|
that changed, stores them, and reads them all back to check.|
'OK' leaves. If a change was not saved, it asks first.|
