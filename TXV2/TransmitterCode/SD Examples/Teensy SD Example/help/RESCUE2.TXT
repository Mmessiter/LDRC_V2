Rescue, height hold: the values used when Rescue is set to Hold height, for the bank shown at the top left.||

Hover height: the height, in metres, the model climbs to and holds (0 to 500; with two decimals).||

Height hold P, I and D: how firmly the height is held (0 to 10000 each). Rotorflight's defaults suit most models; change them in small steps.||
