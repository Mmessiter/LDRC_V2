What rescue does once the model is level: climb, or climb|
and hold a height. For the bank shown at the top left.||
Rescue mode: tap it to step through 'Off', 'Level & climb'|
and 'Level & hold height'.|
Off: no rescue (as 'Enable rescue' off on the first page).|
Level & climb: levels the model, climbs for the climb time,|
then hovers on the hover collective.|
Level & hold height: levels it, climbs to the hold height and|
holds it there. It needs the flight controller's altitude|
sensor (barometer).||
Hold height (m) (hover altitude): the height rescue climbs to|
and holds in 'Level & hold height' (0 to 500, two decimals).|
Height hold P, I and D (altitude P, I and D gain): how firmly|
the height is held (0 to 10000 each). 'Level & hold height'|
only. Rotorflight's defaults suit most models: change them in|
small steps.|
Max collective (%): the most collective rescue may ever use|
(1 to 100, one decimal).||
Tap a number to type a new one.|
'Save' appears once something is changed. It writes both rescue|
pages to the flight controller, stores them and reads them back.|
'< Previous' goes back to the first page, with your changes|
kept but not yet saved.|
'OK' leaves. With unsaved changes it asks 'Save now?':|
'Save' saves them, and the page closes once saved;|
'Discard' leaves without saving.|
