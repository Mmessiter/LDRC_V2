What rescue does once the model is level: climb, or climb|
and hold a height. For the bank shown at the top left.||
Rescue mode: tap it to step through Off, Climb and Hold height.|
Off: no rescue (as Enable rescue off on the first page).|
Climb: levels the model, climbs for the climb time, then|
hovers on the hover collective.|
Hold height: levels it, climbs to the hover height and holds|
it there. It needs the flight controller's altitude sensor|
(barometer).||
Hover height [m]: the height that Hold height climbs to and|
holds (0 to 500, two decimals). Hold height only.|
Height hold P, I and D: how firmly the height is held|
(0 to 10000 each). Hold height only. Rotorflight's defaults|
suit most models: change them in small steps.|
Max collective [%]: the most collective rescue may ever use|
(1 to 100, one decimal).||
Tap a number to type a new one.|
'Save' appears once something is changed. It writes both rescue|
pages to the flight controller, stores them and reads them back.|
'< Previous' goes back to the first page, with your changes|
kept but not yet saved.|
'OK' leaves; if a change was not saved, it asks first.|
