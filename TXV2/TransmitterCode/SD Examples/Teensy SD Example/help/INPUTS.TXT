Choose what moves each channel, give it a name,|
and choose the receiver output it goes to.|
Touch a number or a name to change it.|
'OK' keeps the changes and goes back to 'Model setup'.|
With the model connected, you are asked first:|
changes here move its controls.||
In: the stick, switch or knob that moves this channel.|
Name: the channel's name (up to 10 letters).|
Other pages show it too.|
Out: the receiver output this channel goes to.|
Trim out: which channels each trim moves (see TRIMS).||
By default each channel takes the input and the output|
of its own number. This is Futaba's order (AETR).||
INPUTS:|
1 = aileron stick, 2 = elevator stick,|
3 = throttle stick, 4 = rudder stick,|
in Mode 1 and Mode 2 alike.|
5 to 8 = the front switches or knobs.|
9 to 16 have no stick or knob of their own.||
To use input 9 to 16, give a switch the job 'Channel 9'|
(and so on): 'Transmitter setup', 'Switches'.|
That switch is then input 9, for any channel to take as its In.|
(If you renamed channel 9, the job shows its name instead.)|
A switch given the job 'Channel 5' to 'Channel 8'|
takes the place of that front switch or knob.|
The top-edge switches often have other jobs:|
banks, motor, safety, rates or buddy.|
Channels 9 to 16 are also useful for mixes.||
One input may move several channels (two aileron servos, say).|
Each of them can be reversed on its own.||
OUTPUTS:|
Each Out number may be used only once.|
If two channels share one, every Out number|
goes back to 1 to 16 when you press 'OK'.|
To move a channel to another output, swap two Out numbers:|
for example Ch 4 Out 5 and Ch 5 Out 4.|
Swap in pairs only. Three or more moved round in a ring|
do not go where their Out numbers say.|
To move one output from two controls, use a mix ('Mixes').||
To check which channel moves, watch the 'Channels' page|
while you move a stick, switch or knob.||
TRIMS:|
Most models keep the Trim out numbers at 1, 2, 3, 4.|
Tr1: horizontal trim, under the aileron stick.|
Tr2: left vertical trim; Mode 1 elevator, Mode 2 throttle.|
Tr3: right vertical trim; Mode 1 throttle, Mode 2 elevator.|
Tr4: horizontal trim, under the rudder stick.|
A trim moves every channel whose In is its Trim out number,|
so two aileron servos both take the aileron trim.|
(In Mode 2, Trim out 2 acts on In 3, and 3 acts on In 2.)|
For example, set Tr1 to 14:|
the aileron trim then moves channel 14 instead.|
Its step is the 'Trim multiplier' ('Model setup', 'Options').||
After any change here, TEST ON THE GROUND before flying!|
