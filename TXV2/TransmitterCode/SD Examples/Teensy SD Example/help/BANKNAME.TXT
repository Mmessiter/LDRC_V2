Give each of this model's banks (flight modes) a name for what|
it does. There are four, or six with 'Six banks' on 'Model|
options'. The name shows on the front screen and other pages,|
and is spoken when you change bank.||
TO NAME THE BANKS:|
- Scroll each bank's list to the name you want.|
- Or press a set: 'Default' (Flight mode 1 to 6), 'Vbar'|
(Bank 1 to 6), 'Heli' (Hover, Idle up 1, Idle up 2, Hold,|
Bank 5, Bank 6) or 'Glider' (Launch, Normal, Speed, Thermal,|
Bank 5, Bank 6).|
- 'Listen' plays the names in turn.|
- Press 'OK' to save.||
The names come from a fixed list: each has its own spoken sound.|
To silence them, turn off 'Bank voice' ('Transmitter setup',|
then 'Sounds etc.'). That also silences the safety, motor|
and rates voices.|
