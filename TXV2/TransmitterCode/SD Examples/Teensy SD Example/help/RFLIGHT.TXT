Rotorflight options: the pages that view and edit a flight controller running Rotorflight (2.2 or later), over the receiver's Bluetooth. Do the first setup of the helicopter with Rotorflight's Configurator; at the field, tune it from here with no laptop.||

PIDs, Rates (each with an Advanced page), Governor, Rescue and Servos read their values from the flight controller when opened, for the bank shown at the top left (change bank with the bank switch), and Save writes them back, stores them, and reads them back to check.||

Battery, Black box and Calibrate set the flight pack, the flight recorder and the level. Copy a bank copies the tuning of one bank into another.||

The line under the buttons says whether the flight controller will arm, and if not, why ("Will not arm: Throttle is not at idle"). Tap it for every reason at once. It shows only with the model connected and Bluetooth joined.||

Settings holds what the transmitter itself needs to know: whether rates follow the banks, the Rotorflight version (as the receiver reports it), the arming channel, and the main rotor to motor RPM ratio.||

Command line is Rotorflight's own command line on the screen, through the USB cable from the receiver to the flight controller. Touch the line to type a command on the screen's keys; what the flight controller prints scrolls in the box (drag it with a finger). Type save to keep what you set, or exit to leave it; either restarts the flight controller. OK leaves the page, and asks first if the command line is still open. The model cannot arm while the command line is open.||

Backup / Restore (at the foot) keeps EVERY Rotorflight setting of the model on the transmitter's card, over Bluetooth, and puts them back; it also keeps the flight controller's settings as text (Diff to card) and replays them (Execute diff). With no model connected, the pages read and edit the backup instead, and the edits are offered to the model when it is next connected. Back up as soon as you are happy with a setup.||

Without a model connected, the pages read and edit the model's backup on the card (made with Backup / Restore); Calibrate, the black box's memory and the arming line need the model itself.||

The blue tooth at the right means the link to the receiver is up; the words say when it is connecting, or absent. Land and disarm before using these pages: with the safety off, nothing here will open.||
