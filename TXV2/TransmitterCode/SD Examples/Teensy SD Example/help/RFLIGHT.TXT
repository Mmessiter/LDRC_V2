Rotorflight options: the pages that view and edit a flight controller running Rotorflight (2.2 or later), over the receiver's Bluetooth. Do the first setup of the helicopter with Rotorflight's Configurator; at the field, tune it from here with no laptop.||

PIDs, Rates (each with an Advanced page), Governor, Rescue and Servos read their values from the flight controller when opened, for the bank shown at the top left (change bank with the bank switch), and Save writes them back, stores them, and reads them back to check.||

Settings holds what the transmitter itself needs to know: whether rates follow the banks, the Rotorflight version (as the receiver reports it), the arming channel, and the main rotor to motor RPM ratio.||

Backup / Restore saves the PIDs and rates of a bank to the transmitter's card, and puts them back. Save as soon as you are happy with them; never restore what was never saved.||

Without a model connected, PIDs and Rates show and edit the copies on the card. The other pages need the model.||

The blue tooth at the right means the link to the receiver is up; the words say when it is connecting, or absent. Land and disarm before using these pages: with the safety off, nothing here will open.||
