Tap a number to type a new one.|
'Save' appears once something is changed: it writes all the|
Mixer pages, after a question.|
'Next >' goes on to the tail rotor, '< Previous' back to the|
first Mixer page, changes kept.|
'OK' leaves; if a change was not saved, it asks first.||
ROLL AND PITCH TOGETHER:|
Cyclic calibration and Cyclic pitch limit are each one value|
for both roll and pitch, as in the configurator.|
Leaving this page, or saving on it, gives roll and pitch the|
same value. The next 'Save' writes it to both, even if they|
were set differently before. (Where they differ, this page|
shows the roll calibration and the pitch limit.)||
CALIBRATION:|
Cyclic calibration and Collective calibration [%]: how much of|
a full stick reaches the swash (0 to 1000, one decimal).|
Geometry correction [%]: the configurator's Collective geometry|
correction. It evens out the collective's arc, so up and down|
give the same pitch (-25 to 25, one decimal).||
LIMITS:|
Cyclic pitch limit and Collective pitch limit [deg]: the most|
blade pitch each may give, either way (0 to 30, one decimal).|
Total pitch limit [deg]: cyclic and collective together|
(0 to 36; 0 = off). When on, Rotorflight keeps it at least|
6 degrees above the collective limit: a 'Save' may raise it.||
SWASH:|
Swash phase angle [deg]: turns the cyclic a little round the|
mast (-180 to 180, one decimal).|
Positive and Negative tilt corr. [%]: the configurator's|
Collective tilt correction, for a swash that tilts as the|
collective moves (-100 to 100, whole numbers).|
