Cyclic calibration and Collective calibration [%]: how much of a full stick reaches the swash (0 to 1000). Geometry correction [%]: the configurator's Collective Geometry Correction, evens out the collective's arc (-50 to 50, to one decimal).||

Cyclic pitch limit and Collective pitch limit [deg]: the most cyclic and collective pitch the swash may give, either way (0 to 30). Total pitch limit [deg]: collective and cyclic together; 0 = off. Rotorflight raises it to at least the collective limit plus six degrees when it stores.||

Swash phase angle [deg]: turns the cyclic a little round the mast. Positive and Negative tilt correction [%]: the configurator's Collective Tilt Correction, for a swash that tilts with collective.||
