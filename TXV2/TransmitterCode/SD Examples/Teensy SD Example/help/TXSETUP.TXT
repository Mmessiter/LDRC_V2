This screen offers all options for setting up the transmitter.||

Use the 'Calibrate' option first, and after making any major changes such as replacing the gimbals. ||

Only the first 8 channels, the edge switches and the hardware trims need to be calibrated.||

To setup a model, use the Model setup screen.

To return to the Front screen, press 'OK'.||

WIFI SETUP: choose your network and type its password, once. At the field, your phone's hotspot will do.||

APPEARANCE: the background picture, the themes (each a panel colour with its text colour), and your own front screen. Its 'Help' says more.||

TRANSMITTER UPDATES: asks messiter.com for the latest version. Nothing is installed unless you press 'Install now'. Your models, settings and logs are copied to the screen's card first.||

'Earlier versions' puts a previous version back.||

'Transmitter updates' updates the transmitter and its screen only, and is greyed out while a model is connected. The safety must be on (with no safety switch: the motor off).||

The receiver's own update is on the Model setup screen: 'Receiver updates', available while a model is connected.||

WiFi and Bluetooth switch themselves off while the safety is off (with no safety switch: while the motor is on).||

If new firmware reads a model's settings differently from the old one, it is not kept: the old firmware comes back by itself, and the screen says so.
