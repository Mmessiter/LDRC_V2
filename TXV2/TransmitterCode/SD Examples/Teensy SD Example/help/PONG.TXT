Pong: a game for a day too wet to fly|
('Raining' on Transmitter setup).|
Move each stick up and down to move the paddle on its side.|
The first player to score 10 points wins.|
A new game then starts by itself.|
Press 'OK' to stop playing and go to the front screen.||
Pong will not start while a model is connected|
('The model is connected. Switch it off to play Pong.').|
The radio is off while you play.|
