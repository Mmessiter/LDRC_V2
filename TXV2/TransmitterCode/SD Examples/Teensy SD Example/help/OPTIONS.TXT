System: three pages of settings for the transmitter itself.|
Press 'OK' on each page you change:|
it saves that page and goes back to Transmitter setup.|
'Next >' and '< Back' move between pages, but may lose changes.||
GENERAL (1 OF 3):|
'Automatic model selection (AMS)': when a model connects, the model|
memory that holds its ID is loaded.|
If none holds it, you are asked whether to store|
it with the model now loaded.|
'Cancel' stores nothing, and the model does not connect.|
With AMS off, the loaded memory stays, whichever model connects.|
So load the right model memory before you switch the model on.|
Store each model's ID on Model setup > 'Model IDs'.||
'Screen goes dark after' (seconds):|
the screen turns off to save battery, even in flight.|
Touch the screen anywhere to switch it back on.|
'Switch off when unused for' (5 to 60 minutes):|
switches off a transmitter left on by mistake.|
It never switches off while a model is connected.|
Touching the screen starts the count again;|
moving a stick does not.|
'Transmitter battery low at' (10 to 100 %):|
warns you when the transmitter battery falls this low.|
To be safe, set it quite high.|
The model's warning is 'Stop flying volts (per cell)'|
on Model setup > 'Options'.|
'Stick mode' (1 or 2): set this first.|
After a change, calibrate the sticks and the trims again.|
'Owner's name': shown at the top left of the front screen.||
BINDING (PAIRING A RECEIVER WITH THIS TRANSMITTER):|
1. Get the receiver ready to bind:|
- a new Version 2 receiver is ready already;|
- a bound Version 2 receiver:|
'Clear bind & restart' on its 'Bind' page (phone app or web page);|
- a Version 1 receiver: switch it on with its bind plug in.|
2. With no model connected, press 'Bind'|
(it changes to 'Stop binding').|
Or keep holding the power button as you switch on:|
after 2 seconds it says 'Binding enabled' (loud enough|
even with the volume at 0) and every front screen shows it.|
Holding on longer is safe: a hold kept from switch-on|
never switches off. Let go: the light blinks while binding is on.|
3. The receiver binds and connects.|
Binding then stops by itself.|
A bound receiver connects only to its own transmitter.||
CLOCK (2 OF 3):|
'+' and '-' change each part of the date and time, at once.|
The clock also sets itself:|
- from the internet when the screen is on WiFi|
(UK time, summer time included);|
- from your phone, through a Version 2 receiver;|
- from GPS in the model (GMT).|
'Time zone (hours from GMT)': hours added to the time shown|
here, on the front screen and in the logs.|
GPS sets the clock to GMT: with GPS, set your time zone here.|
Leave it at 0 if the clock already keeps your local time.|
'Reset clock' sets the clock to the date|
and time this firmware was built.|
You are asked first.||
ADVANCED (3 OF 3):|
'Keep log files': writes a log of each session on the card.|
Read it on Model setup > 'Log'.|
'Log receiver radio swaps':|
logs each time the receiver changes to another of its radios.|
'Log gaps longer than' (10 to 255 ms):|
logs each gap in the link this long or longer.|
These two work only with 'Keep log files' on.||
'LED brightness' (15 to 254): the transmitter's coloured light.|
'Transmitter battery correction' (0.01 V per cell, 0 to 20):|
if the transmitter battery reads lower than an accurate|
voltmeter, enter the difference per cell.|
The model battery's correction is on Model setup > 'Options'.|
'Hold power button for' (1 to 10 seconds): with a model|
connected, hold the power button this long to switch off.|
The front screen counts down.|
With no model connected it switches off at once.|
'Connection quality measured over': has no effect now.|
The quality bar always counts the last 200 data packets.|
'Radio scanner sensitivity' (1 to 255):|
how slowly the bars of 'Scan RF' fall back.|
Higher keeps them up longer.|
'Sea-level pressure (QNH)' (hPa):|
today's air pressure at sea level where you fly.|
It makes altitude readings from a BMP280 barometer in the model more|
accurate, if one is fitted (a Version 2 receiver has none).|
Aviation web sites give it for nearby airports.|
