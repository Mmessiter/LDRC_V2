To add your own photo:|
1. Press Add photo.|
2. Scan the code with your phone's camera.|
3. Choose a photo and fit it in the frame.|
4. Press Send, then OK on the transmitter.||

The phone must be on the same WiFi as the transmitter. The phone makes the photo the right size.||

Your own photos have green names. Delete removes one of yours.||

All pictures are kept on the screen's card. They are not needed on the Teensy's card any more.|
