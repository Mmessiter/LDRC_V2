The curve sets how far this channel's servo moves (up the graph)|
for each stick position (across). The channel is named at the top|
right, with the bank under it.||
TO CHANGE THE CURVE:|
- Touch the graph above or below a point: the point moves there.|
- For small steps, press 'Next' until the ring is on the point|
you want, then press '-' or '+'.|
- 'Flat': every point takes the ringed point's height, for a|
level line (a governed throttle at full, for instance).|
- 'Reset': the curve as new - a straight line from bottom left|
to top right, and no expo. The shape and the points stay chosen.|
- Choose the shape: 'Straight', 'Smooth' or 'Exponential'.|
- Choose how many points: 'Points: 3 5 7 9' (see below).|
- Turn on 'Copy to all banks' to copy this curve to every bank|
(four, or six with 'Six banks' on) when you press 'OK'.|
- Press 'OK' to save.|
Each bank has its own curve.|
Move the bank switch to change another bank's curve.||
THE NUMBERS (microseconds):|
Along the top: each point's height (middle 1500). The graph's|
bottom and top are the model's max range (on the Channels page).|
Two numbers turn green when their points sit the same distance|
above and below the centre point: that pair is symmetrical.|
The upright line is where the stick is now.|
'Input': where the stick is, on the usual 1000 to 2000 scale.|
'Output': what this channel sends now, with rates, trims and|
mixes (as on the Channels page).|
For Rotorflight on CRSF, ends at 1000 and 2000 use the whole|
stick: it reads nothing beyond them.||
HOW MANY POINTS:|
'Points: 3 5 7 9' sets how many points this channel's curve has,|
in every bank (it shows for Straight and Smooth). The ends and|
the centre are always points; the rest are spaced evenly between|
them. Every point keeps its height: change the count and back,|
and the points return as you left them. A point you have never|
set is placed on the curve as it is, so nothing moves. More|
points give finer control. The choice is saved at once.|
Exponential always uses three.||
THE SHAPES:|
1. Straight: straight lines join the points.|
2. Smooth: a smooth curve through the points.|
3. Exponential: three points (the ends and the centre) joined|
by two expo curves. Only those three points can be moved, and|
'Next' skips the others.|
Set 'Expo' (-50 to +150) with the slider, or with the '-' and '+'|
beside it for single steps. Expo 0 gives straight lines.|
The slider is hard to stop at exactly 0, so 'Expo 0' sets 0|
for you (and chooses 'Exponential').|
