Sounds and brightness: what the transmitter says, how loud|
it is, how bright the screen is, and the vario.|
Touch a switch to turn a sound on or off.|
The sliders work at once. 'OK' keeps all your choices.||
VOICES AND SOUNDS:|
'Timer voice': says each minute of the motor timer, up to 10.|
'Bank voice': says the bank or rate when you change it.|
It also says motor on / off and safety on / off.|
'Connected voice': says when the model connects and disconnects.|
'Start and close sounds': a tune as you switch on and off.|
'Trim change clicks': a click for each trim step.|
A different beep marks the centre and the end.||
VARIO (VARIOMETER):|
Tones that tell you whether the model is climbing or sinking.|
Climbing, they are sine waves (they sound nice).|
Sinking, they are sawtooth waves (they do not).|
'Vario tones': switches the vario on.|
'Vario in bank' (0 = all): the bank it sounds in.|
Type 1, 2 or 3, or 0 for every bank.|
'First tone at' (ft/min, 0 to 1000):|
the slowest climb or sink that makes a sound.|
Slower is silent.|
'Next tone every' (ft/min, 50 to 1000):|
how much faster the climb or sink must be for the next tone.|
There are ten tones each way.|
The vario needs a pressure sensor in the model's receiver:|
a BMP280, or better a DPS310.|
A Version 2 receiver has neither,|
so with one the vario stays silent.|
It starts 10 seconds after the model connects.||
SCREEN AND VOLUME:|
'Screen brightness' sets the screen.|
Its lowest is 10, so it never makes the screen fully dark.|
'Volume' sets every sound.|
