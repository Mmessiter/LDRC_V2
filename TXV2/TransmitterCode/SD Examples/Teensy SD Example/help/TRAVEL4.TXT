The bench measure: the swash held at an exact angle, so a|
pitch gauge can check the real blade angles.|
It is the phone app's 'Bench measure' on its Travel extents|
page, and Rotorflight's own way to set the cyclic gain.||
MOTOR OFF, BLADES CLEAR. It moves the swash.|
It needs the model itself, switched on, joined by Bluetooth|
and disarmed (safety on). Its backup on the card cannot do it.||
HOW TO USE IT:|
1. Axis: tap to choose Roll (aileron) or Pitch (elevator).|
2. Angle (deg): the angle to hold, up to 12 degrees either way.|
   8 is a good one.|
3. 'Hold swash': the swash goes to that angle, the gyros|
   bypassed, the other two swash controls held at zero.|
   The top line says SWASH HELD, and the button says 'Release'.|
   Tap Axis while held: the swash goes over to the other axis.|
4. Put the gauge on one blade and turn the head slowly by|
   hand: note the HIGHEST reading anywhere round the circle.|
5. Peak gauge reading (deg): type that reading.|
6. Tap the Suggested gain (%) box: it works out the gain that|
   makes the gauge agree with the angle (gain x angle /|
   gauge reading), and puts it in the Cyclic gain (%) box.|
   'Save' then writes it.|
7. 'Release' lets the swash go. Hold again to check.||
Cyclic gain (%) is the same box as on 'Travel extents: swash':|
it shows roll's gain, and a new value goes to roll and pitch.|
A 'Save' here writes all four Travel extents pages, after a|
question.||
THE HOLD LETS GO:|
by 'Release', when you leave with 'OK' or '< Previous', and by|
itself after five minutes. Rotorflight obeys a hold only while|
disarmed: once armed, the sticks rule. If the link is lost|
while held and the swash stays put, switch the flight|
controller off and on.||
COLLECTIVE is not measured here. Set it the direct way:|
full stick, measure, then adjust the Collective gain (%) on|
'Travel extents: swash'.|
