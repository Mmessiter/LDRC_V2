Is the flight controller the right way round?||
1. Stand the model level and press 'Start'.|
2. Tilt the nose down about 30 degrees, then level again.|
3. Tilt the right side down, then level again.|
4. Turn the nose to the right (keep it level).||
Each move is marked green when the flight controller saw it|
the right way, red when it saw it backwards or about another|
axis. The yellow line shows what it sees, all the time.||
TURNED ROUND: if the nose and the side both come out|
backwards (and the turn is right), the board's connections|
face the other way. 'Turn it round' sets the other of the two|
mountings (connections to the front or to the back), stores|
it and restarts the flight controller. Then check again.||
Any other result is a custom mounting: set it with the|
configurator. Never fly until all three are green.|
