Slow a servo down, for flaps, airbrakes or undercarriage.||
Touch a channel's box and type its speed, from 1 to 100.|
100 = full speed (no slowing). 1 = the slowest.|
Most channels stay at 100.|
Halve the number, and the move takes twice as long.|
With the usual end points, 10 takes about|
one and a half seconds from end to end.||
Use 1 to 100 only. A 0, or more than 100,|
is put back to 100 by itself.||
Each bank has its own speeds. The bank in use is shown|
top left. Move the bank switch to set another bank's speeds.||
'Reset' puts this bank's speeds back to 100.|
'OK' keeps the changes and goes back to 'Model setup'.|
