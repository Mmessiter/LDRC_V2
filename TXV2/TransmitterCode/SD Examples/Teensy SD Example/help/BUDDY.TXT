Wireless buddy: dual control, for teaching beginners to fly.|
The Instructor (Master) and the Pupil (Buddy) fly one model.|
The Instructor's buddy switch gives the Pupil control.|
Move it back, and the Instructor flies again at once.|
Only the Instructor's transmitter talks to the model.|
The Pupil's talks to the Instructor's.||
SET UP THE INSTRUCTOR'S TRANSMITTER:|
1. Connect the model. On Model setup > 'Model IDs',|
press 'Store this model's ID'.|
2. Back up the model with '> Backup >' on the 'Models' page.|
Then 'Send' it to the Pupil (see the 'Models' help).|
The file carries the model's ID and the Instructor's ID.|
So store the model's ID BEFORE you send the model.|
3. Give a switch the job 'Buddy' on the 'Switches' page.|
A spring-return switch is best: let go to take control back.|
4. Here, turn 'Instructor (Master)' on.|
Under 'Buddy switch', choose who flies at Down, Middle and Up:|
'Master', 'Nudge' or 'Buddy'.|
5. 'Buddy channels ...': turn on the channels the Pupil may fly.|
'All' and 'None' set every channel, and 'Top switches too'.|
The others stay with the Instructor, whoever has control.|
Each model keeps its own choice.||
SET UP THE PUPIL'S TRANSMITTER:|
Receive the model from the Instructor into the memory you chose.|
Then turn 'Pupil (Buddy)' on here.||
On either transmitter, 'OK' saves and goes to the front screen.|
If 'Middle' and 'Up' are both 'Master', you are asked to check.||
THE TOP SWITCHES:|
On the Buddy channels page,|
'Top switches too (motor, safety, bank and rates)' decides whose|
switches count while the Pupil has control.|
For a beginner, leave it OFF:|
the Instructor keeps motor on / off, safety, bank and rates.|
A more advanced pupil, perhaps learning autorotations,|
will need these switches too.|
When the Instructor flies, the Instructor's switches count.||
NUDGE:|
With 'Nudge' the Pupil flies, but the Instructor's sticks can gently|
nudge the model, rather than take over.|
Not on channel 3 (collective / throttle):|
that stick does not self-centre.||
AMS:|
If AMS (automatic model selection) is on in both transmitters,|
and both have the model's ID stored,|
BOTH load the right model by themselves,|
even if one had the wrong model loaded.||
WHO HAS CONTROL:|
When the buddy switch moves, both transmitters say who flies:|
'Master', 'Buddy' or 'Buddy with nudge'.|
The Instructor's front screen shows|
'Buddy found!' or 'Buddy missing'.|
The Pupil's shows 'Master found!' or 'Master missing'.|
If the Pupil's signal is lost, the Instructor gets control back|
by itself after about 5 seconds.|
To take it back at once, use the buddy switch.|
'Extra buddies!' (Pupil's screen): a second buddy is on the air.|
Use one Pupil at a time.||
WIRED BUDDY:|
There is no wired buddy: wireless is much simpler and better.|
