It needs the safety on (model disarmed) and a Version 2|
receiver with Bluetooth. With no model, the page shows the|
model's backup on the card, if one was made: a 'Save' then|
changes the backup, and the change is offered to the model|
when it next connects.||
Tap a number to type a new one.|
Tap a switch to change it (green = on).|
'< Servo' and 'Servo >' step through the servos. The title says|
which one you are on, and its job: 'Servo 1 of 4 (swashplate)'.|
Servos 1, 2 and 3 are usually the swashplate, 4 the tail.|
'Save' appears once something is changed. It writes this servo|
to the flight controller, stores it there and reads it back.|
Save each servo before you move on: a change not saved is|
dropped (it asks first).|
'OK' leaves. With a change not saved it asks 'Save now?':|
'Save' saves, then leaves; 'Discard' leaves without saving.||
THE SETTINGS:|
Centre (subtrim) (us): where the arm sits with the sticks|
centred, in microseconds (750 to 2250). Adjust it so the|
linkages are level.|
Travel limit low and high (us): the stops (-1000 to 0, and|
0 to 1000). The servo never moves past them.|
Throw low side and high side (us): the arm movement a full|
stick asks for, each way (50 to 1000). Usually both the same.|
Update rate (Hz): how often the servo is sent a new position|
(25 to 5000). Never more than the servo's specification allows.|
Slow-motion (ms): slows the servo down. It is the time the arm|
takes to move 60 degrees (0 to 60000). 0 = full speed (normal).|
Reversed: turns this servo the other way, like a servo-reverse|
switch. To reverse a control (aileron, elevator, collective or|
rudder), use 'Travel extents ...' on the Rotorflight page.|
Geometry correction: evens out the arc of the servo arm, so|
equal commands give equal swash movement up and down.|
Usually on for the three swashplate servos.|
Not the same as 'Up/down balance' on the Travel extents pages.|
