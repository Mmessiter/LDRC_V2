The rest of the flight controller's PID settings, for the bank|
shown at the top left. The names are the phone app's.|
Rotorflight's own names are in brackets below|
(in the Configurator: the Profiles tab).|
Most models fly well on Rotorflight's own values.|
Change one only when you know why.||
TO CHANGE THEM:|
1. Choose the bank with the bank switch. Wait while it loads.|
2. Touch a value, or its name, and type the new value.|
3. Press 'Save' (it appears once you touch a value).|
The values are written to the flight controller,|
read back to check, and stored.||
CHECK THE NAME:|
The keypad shows the name of the value you are changing.|
Check it: the wrong one is easy to touch on this crowded page.|
If it is the wrong one, press 'Cancel': nothing changes.||
GOOD TO KNOW:|
Save before you move the switch: a move loads the other bank,|
and unsaved changes are lost.|
To check a save, move the switch away and back.|
The page reads the values again.|
If the page says nothing came back, do the same,|
or leave the page and open it again.|
'Save' writes only into the transmitter's bank.|
'Bank 2 / FC 1' at the top left: the flight controller is on|
bank 1. The page shows bank 1, but 'Save' writes nothing, and|
a message says so. Check the bank switch's set-up.|
(The check needs Bluetooth joined.)||
THE SETTINGS:|
Piro compensation: a switch. Touch it to turn it on or off.|
Relax on the ground (ground error decay): on the ground,|
built-up corrections drain away, so the swash does not lean.|
Roll, Pitch and Yaw each have:|
- wind-up relief (I-term relax cutoff): holds back the I term|
in fast moves, so it cannot snap back. Lower: more relief.|
- correction cap (error limit): the biggest error, in degrees,|
that it will try to wind away.|
- response speed (bandwidth, Hz): higher is crisper,|
but more sensitive to vibration.|
- D smoothing (D-term cutoff, Hz): lower it if servos buzz|
or run hot.|
- boost smoothing (B-term cutoff, Hz): the filter on Boost.|
Roll and Pitch trim cap (HSI offset limit): the most the|
automatic trim may add in fast forward flight, in degrees.|
How strongly it builds up is 'HSI gain' on the PIDs page.|
Yaw stop firmness CW and CCW (CW and CCW stop gain): how|
firmly a yaw stops when you centre the stick.|
Raise the side that wags or bounces.|
Yaw precomp smoothing (precomp cutoff, Hz): smoothing of the|
yaw pushes below.|
Yaw push from cyclic and from collective (cyclic and|
collective FF gain): tail added as that load rises, before|
the nose can swing. Collective is the main one.|
Yaw push from speed-ups (inertia precomp gain): tail added|
while the rotor speeds up or slows down.|
Speed-up push smoothing (inertia precomp cutoff, Hz):|
smoothing of that push.||
THE NUMBERS:|
'Relax on the ground' and 'Speed-up push smoothing' have|
one decimal place (0.0 to 25.5).|
All the others are whole numbers, 0 to 255.||
'OK' leaves the page. With unsaved changes it asks 'Save now?':|
'Save' saves them, and the page closes once saved;|
'Discard' leaves without saving.|
With no model connected the page shows the model's backup|
(see the help on the 'Rotorflight' page).|
