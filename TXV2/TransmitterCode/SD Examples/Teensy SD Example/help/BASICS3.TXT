The flight controller's serial ports (UARTs), and what each|
one does: the configurator's Ports tab.||
Each row is a socket. On a RadioMaster Nexus the letters are|
the ones printed on the board: Port A is UART 4, Port B is|
UART 6, Port C is UART 3. On the Nexus-X, TELEM is UART 2 and|
S.BUS is UART 1; on the original Nexus, S.BUS is UART 2 and|
DSM is UART 1. Other boards show UART numbers only.||
Tap a row to step its job: None, ESC telemetry, GPS, Dongle|
(MSP 115200: an LDRC dongle on a wire), Telemetry (S.Port),|
Blackbox. 'Other (as set)' is a job this page does not know;|
a tap takes it to None.|
'Receiver (this link)' is the port your receiver is wired to:|
it cannot be changed here. Moved, the transmitter would lose|
the flight controller. To move it, use the phone app's 'Ports'|
page, with a USB-C cable from the receiver to the flight|
controller.||
'Save' writes all the Model basics pages, adds the features a|
job needs (ESC telemetry, GPS, telemetry), stores them and|
restarts the flight controller. It asks first.|
A port given a job it is not wired for is simply silent.|
The ports need the model itself: its backup keeps no ports.|
'< Previous' goes back to the gear ratios, 'Next >' on to the|
ESC throttle calibration.|
'OK' leaves; if a change was not saved, it asks first.|
