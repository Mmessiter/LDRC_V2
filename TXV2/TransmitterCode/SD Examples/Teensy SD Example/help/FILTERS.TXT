Gyro filters: Rotorflight's filtering of the gyro signal, laid out as the configurator's Gyro tab, with its names. Needs a Version 2 receiver with Bluetooth, the safety on and the model disarmed. The same for every bank. Next > shows what the configurator's expert mode adds.||

Lowpass Filter: smooths everything above the cutoff. Enable off = no lowpass (the type and cutoff are kept for an Enable on). Filter type: 1st order or 2nd order (tap to change; another type set from the configurator stays offered while it is the one set). Cutoff frequency: Rotorflight advises not below 60 Hz; 100 is usual.||

Dynamic Filter: notches that find the strongest vibrations by themselves. Notch count 0 to 8 (4 to 6 alone, 2 to 4 with the RPM filter). Notch Q: how narrow each notch is, 2.0 to 4.0 recommended, below 2.0 adds delay. Notch minimum and maximum: the range they search, in Hz.||

RPM Filter: follows the head speed and removes the rotor and motor vibrations wherever they are; the main filter on a helicopter. It needs a head-speed signal (ESC telemetry or an RPM sensor). Strength: Low, Medium or High (more removes more, with more delay); Custom keeps a table made in the configurator. Minimum frequency: set a little below the main rotor's frequency at the lowest flying head speed (20 Hz is usual).||

The Enable of the Dynamic Filter and of the RPM Filter are Rotorflight features, which take effect only when the flight controller starts: a save that changes one restarts the flight controller (as the configurator does), and the page waits for it and reads back.||

Press a number to type a new one. Save writes to the flight controller, stores there, and reads back. OK leaves; if something was edited and not saved, it asks.||
