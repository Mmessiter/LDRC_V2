What Rotorflight does when you flick the rescue switch:|
it takes over, levels the model and climbs.|
Names as on the phone app's 'Rescue' page. Order and units|
as in the Configurator (its names in brackets below).||
It needs the safety on (model disarmed) and a Version 2|
receiver with Bluetooth. With no model, the page shows the|
model's backup on the card, if one was made: a 'Save' then|
changes the backup, and the change is offered to the model|
when it next connects.||
Tap a number to type a new one.|
Tap a switch to change it (green = on).|
'Next >' opens the second page, 'Rescue: hold height', with|
your changes kept: Rescue mode, Hold height, Height hold P,|
I and D, and Max collective.|
'Save' appears once something is changed. It writes both|
rescue pages to the flight controller, stores them there and|
reads them back, so the page shows what it now holds.|
'OK' leaves. With unsaved changes it asks 'Save now?':|
'Save' saves them, and the page closes once saved;|
'Discard' leaves without saving.||
BANKS:|
The values are those of the bank shown at the top left.|
Move the bank switch and the page reads that bank. Save|
first: changes not saved are lost (a message says so).||
BEFORE YOU RELY ON IT:|
Rescue also needs a switch: set one with 'Switches ...' on|
the 'Rotorflight' page (Rescue - the safety net).|
Calibrate the level first too ('Calibrate ...'): rescue|
levels the model by it.|
Test rescue at a safe height before you trust it low down.||
WHAT RESCUE DOES, STAGE BY STAGE:|
1. Pull-up: it levels the model, on the pull-up collective.|
2. Flip: with 'Flip upright if inverted' on, a model that is|
upside down is turned the right way up.|
3. Climb: on the climb collective.|
'Level & hold height': it climbs towards the hold height.|
4. Hover: on the hover collective, until you let go of the|
switch. 'Level & hold height': it stays at the hold height.|
5. Exit: it hands control back to you over the exit time.|
Let go of the switch at any stage and it goes to Exit.||
THE SETTINGS:|
Enable rescue: rescue on or off. 'Level & climb' or|
'Level & hold height' is chosen on the second page|
(Rescue mode).|
Flip upright if inverted (flip to upright): on, a model that|
is upside down when rescue starts is turned the right way up|
first. Recommended on.|
Pull-up, Climb and Hover collective (%): the collective each|
stage of the rescue uses (0 to 100, one decimal: 65.5).|
Pull-up time and Climb time (s): how long those stages last.|
Flip time (s) (flip fail time): how long it may take to turn|
the model upright.|
Exit time (s): how long it takes to hand control back to you.|
Times: 0 to 25 seconds, one decimal (1.5 = a second and a half).|
Levelling strength and Flip strength (levelling gain and|
flip-to-upright gain): how firmly it levels the model, and|
how hard it rolls it over (5 to 250).|
Max turn rate (max levelling rate): 1 to 1000 degrees a second.|
Max turn acceleration (max levelling accel.): 1 to 10000.|
They limit how fast it may roll the model level.|
