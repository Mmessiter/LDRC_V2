The governor settings shared by every bank:|
its mode, the throttle and the times.|
Each bank's head speed and gains are on the Governor page.|
It needs Rotorflight 2.3.||
TO CHANGE THEM:|
Touch a value, or its name, and type the new value.|
Then press 'Save': it writes the values, checks and stores them.|
Then the flight controller restarts: Rotorflight reads these|
only as it starts.|
'Save' is refused while the model could be armed.|
If a read fails, it goes back to the Governor page: try again.||
GENERAL:|
Governor mode: 0 Off, 1 Limit, 2 Direct, 3 Electric, 4 Nitro.|
The box above it says which, in words.|
Electric is the full governor for an electric helicopter.|
Handover [%]: above this throttle the governor takes over,|
and spools up to the bank's head speed.|
Autorot timeout: in seconds. How long an autorotation keeps|
the quick bail-out ready. 0: no autorotation.||
THROTTLE:|
Throttle type: 0 Normal, 1 Switch, 2 Function, as in|
Rotorflight's Configurator. The box above it says which.|
Idle thr x10: the throttle while idling below handover.|
Auto thr x10: the throttle during an autorotation.|
x10 means tenths of a per cent: 50 is 5 %.||
TIMES:|
In whole seconds. Rotorflight keeps tenths, so a time of|
2.5 s shows as 3, and is saved as 3.|
Spool-up: the time to reach head speed. Higher is gentler.|
Spool-down: the time to wind down when you lower the throttle.|
Tracking: how fast the head speed follows a change,|
such as a bank change.|
The time is for the whole range: a small change is quicker.|
Recovery: the quick spool-up after a short throttle hold.|
Hold timeout: how long the quick spool-up stays ready|
after a throttle hold.|
Startup time and the governor filters are not on this page.|
'Save' keeps them (the startup time in whole seconds).||
A COPY IN THE MODEL'S FILE:|
'Keep a copy' stores the page's values in the model's file|
on the transmitter's card. It asks first.|
It does not send them to the flight controller:|
press 'Save' first if you changed anything.|
'Restore' writes that copy to the connected model's|
flight controller, which then restarts. It asks first.|
This copy is not the backup made with 'Backup / Restore ...'.||
'OK' goes back to the Rotorflight options.|
It asks first if you have unsaved changes.|
No model connected: the page shows the model's backup.|
With no backup either, 'Save' does the same as 'Keep a copy'.|
